Overlap Privacy Policy

App: Overlap – Meeting Time Zones (com.coderhub.overlap) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Overlap is a time zone planner. You add cities, see their clocks, and find meeting times that fit everyone's working hours. Your cities, planner and settings are stored on your phone. The developer does not run a server for Overlap, does not receive your cities, planner or meeting details, and does not sell or share them. Overlap has no accounts and no analytics SDK. It shows ads from Google AdMob, as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Cities you follow, including the favourite flag, and the meeting planner (its name, the cities taking part and the working-hours window). These are kept in the app's private database.
  • Settings such as theme, colour and the remove-ads period. These are kept in the app's private storage. Google's consent platform keeps your ad consent choice in its own private storage.
  • Nothing you enter is sent to the developer. Overlap has no sign-in, so there is no account data.
  • Android backup is turned off for Overlap, so your data is not copied to Google's cloud backup or to a computer.

Time zone calculations

All times and offsets are calculated on your phone from the timezone database that Android provides. Overlap does not send time zone or location data anywhere. Settings shows the version of that database on your device.

Sharing and calendar

  • Share proposal. Overlap creates a short text with each city's local start time and hands it to the share sheet you choose. What happens next is up to the app you share with.
  • Add to calendar. Overlap opens your calendar app with the start time, length, title and details filled in. The calendar app saves the event. Overlap does not read or change your calendar, and it does not ask for calendar permission.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Overlap shows a banner on its home screen through Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested, Overlap uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it. No ad is requested until the consent platform says ads may be requested.

  • Advertising ID. AdMob uses the Android advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your phone's settings, to show ads. The developer does not receive it.
  • Ad requests. To load an ad, AdMob receives standard request information such as your IP address, device and app details. It handles that data under its own policies: Google privacy policy.
  • Privacy options. Where Google's consent platform says a privacy options choice is required, Settings > Privacy shows a Privacy options button that lets you change your consent.
  • Turning the banner off. Watching a rewarded video in Settings hides the banner for 24 hours. A monthly subscription bought through Google Play hides it for as long as the subscription is active. Google Play handles the payment and tells Overlap whether the subscription is active. Overlap does not receive your payment details.

Home-screen widget

The world clock widget shows the local times of up to four of your cities. It reads the same private database and refreshes about every 30 minutes and whenever your cities change. It does not send anything anywhere.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Needed to load ads and to check a subscription with Google Play. Planning does not use the network.
  • Advertising ID. Used by AdMob for ads, as described above.
  • Google Play billing. Used for the optional subscription.

Overlap asks for no runtime permissions: no location, no contacts, no calendar, no microphone and no camera.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes your cities, planner and settings from the phone. Uninstalling Overlap also removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us. Ad-related data held by Google is governed by Google's policies, linked above.

Children

Overlap is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
