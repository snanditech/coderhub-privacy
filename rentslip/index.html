RentSlip Privacy Policy

App: RentSlip (rentslip) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

RentSlip is a private, offline rent record book for tenants and small landlords. It stores tenant details, monthly rent and maintenance, amounts received and payment dates, and generates receipt PDFs and CSV exports. It has no account, no developer server and no INTERNET permission. It is not a tax, HRA or legal-compliance service.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in private storage under files/rentslip:

  • ledger.json (with a local .bak copy and temporary write file): tenants (name, monthly rent, due day, start period, optional PAN, optional phone, premises address); receipts (tenant link, receipt number, month, rent, maintenance, amount paid, payment date, payment mode and note); and internal counters.
  • Private preferences: theme, landlord name, optional landlord PAN, landlord address, revenue-stamp amount and stamp switch.

These are tenant, contact and financial records, and a PAN is personal tax information when supplied. The app does not add its own encryption; the ledger and preferences rely on Android's app sandbox and device storage encryption. There is no redaction option: a PAN you enter is printed or exported in full.

What leaves the device

Nothing is uploaded to the developer. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no developer server, advertising or analytics code.

PDF and CSV exports leave only when you choose Share or a system file-picker destination. The PDF summary and receipts, and the CSV ledger, contain tenant names, addresses, phone numbers, optional PAN, rent, payments and notes. A receiving app or storage provider keeps its own copy under its own policy and may use a network. Cache export copies are app-cache files and may remain until Android clears the cache or you clear the app's storage; shared/saved copies are outside the app.

Permissions

The app declares no Android runtime permission. It does not request storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access. Sharing uses a non-exported FileProvider for the cache exports/ directory; saving uses the Storage Access Framework, so no storage permission is needed.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Android backup and device transfer

Android automatic backup is turned off for this app (the manifest sets allowBackup=false and fullBackupContent=false). Its current extraction rules exclude only the database and sharedpref domains from cloud backup; the app's files/ records are not named in a separate rule. The file also has no device-transfer block. The manifest switch is the primary control, and the extraction XML should be widened if backup is ever enabled.

Financial and legal limits

RentSlip records payments you enter. It does not calculate HRA, decide tax eligibility, verify a tenancy, guarantee legal or tax compliance, or validate a revenue-stamp rule. Revenue-stamp rules differ by state and amount; the stamp box is a placeholder and not legal advice. Reminders are shown inside the app only; no background notification is scheduled. The free-receipt limit and subscription in the portfolio plan are not implemented in this build.

Exports

CSV fields that could start a spreadsheet formula are neutralised. PDF receipts are generated with Android's framework PDF writer. A long tenant name or address wraps in the PDF, but PDF rendering and Devanagari shaping still need a viewer check on a device.

Ads, accounts and purchases

The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no advertising, analytics or billing SDK. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

RentSlip is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Delete a tenant (which also deletes its receipts), delete individual receipts, or use Clear all in Settings. Clearing the app's storage from Android Settings > Apps > RentSlip > Storage also deletes the ledger and preferences. Uninstalling the app deletes its private storage. PDFs/CSVs you shared or saved are outside the app and remain until you delete them. There is no developer server copy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
