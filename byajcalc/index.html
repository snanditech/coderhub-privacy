ByajCalc Privacy Policy

App: ByajCalc (byajcalc) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

ByajCalc is an interest calculator and record book for informal personal lending in India. All interest arithmetic and statements are produced on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Saved loans: borrower or lender name, principal, monthly rate, start date, interest type, part payments and any notes.
  • Settings such as the theme.

Loans are kept in one JSON file and settings in private preferences in the app's storage. Android's automatic backup is turned off for ByajCalc (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

ByajCalc does not add its own encryption to these records; they rely on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

When you share a statement, a PDF is built on the device, written to the app's cache and shared read-only through a FileProvider URI. The PDF contains the names and amounts on the loan. An app you share it to keeps its own copy under its own policy.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Financial records

ByajCalc is a record-keeper, not a lender. It does not arrange loans, check credit, verify payments or send your details to anyone. The figures are based on the rate and dates you enter and are for your own records.

Ads, accounts and purchases

ByajCalc has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

ByajCalc is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling ByajCalc deletes every saved loan and its settings with the app's private storage. You can also delete individual loans in the app, or clear everything from Android Settings > Apps > ByajCalc > Storage > Clear storage. Shared statement PDFs are outside the app and remain until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
