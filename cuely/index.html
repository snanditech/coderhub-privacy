Cuely Privacy Policy

App: Cuely: Offline 8-Ball Pool (com.coderhub.cuely) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Cuely is an offline 8-ball pool game for one phone. You can play against a computer opponent or pass the phone between two players. The developer does not run any server for Cuely, does not receive any data from the app, and does not sell or share data. Cuely has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no in-app purchases. The app does not request the internet permission and cannot send data off your phone.

Information stored on your device

  • Game in progress. The current game is saved in the app's private storage after each shot, so you can resume it after the app is closed. It is removed when a game ends or when you start a new one.
  • Settings. Your choices for theme, sound, vibration, bot strength and the rebound aim preview are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Record. Wins and losses for each kind of game (against the bot at each strength, and pass and play) are counted and kept in the app's private storage. The record is never sent anywhere.

This information stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Permissions

  • Vibration. Short vibrations on a shot and at the end of a game. You can turn this off in Settings.

Cuely does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and it does not request internet access.

Sound

Cuely plays short sound effects for a shot and for a potted ball using the phone's own tone generator. You can turn these off in Settings.

Sharing

Cuely does not share anything. It has no share button and does not send scores anywhere.

Children

Cuely is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not ask for personal information and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Cuely, or clearing the app's data in Android settings, removes everything the app has stored. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
