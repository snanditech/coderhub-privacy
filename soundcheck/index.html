SoundCheck Privacy Policy

App: SoundCheck – Sound Level Meter (com.coderhub.soundcheck) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

SoundCheck is a sound level meter. It shows decibel levels from your phone's microphone and can save a noise log on your phone. Audio is not recorded, not stored and not sent anywhere. The developer does not run a server for SoundCheck, has no accounts, and does not collect analytics or crash reports. The app shows Google ads and sells an optional subscription through Google Play, as described below.

Microphone

  • What it is used for. SoundCheck asks for the microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) to measure the sound level. Before the system permission dialog, the app explains this.
  • When it is used. The microphone is open only while the meter screen or the calibration screen is visible, and while a noise log is recording. When you leave those screens or the app goes to the background, measurement stops. A recording continues in the background only as a foreground service, which shows a persistent notification with a Stop action.
  • What is kept. Audio is processed live on your phone and discarded. SoundCheck does not store audio, and it does not record sound files.
  • Numbers that are kept. During a noise log, SoundCheck saves one level value (in dB) per second, with the time, the weighting (A, C or Z) and any mark you add. Summaries are saved with each log: the average (Leq), minimum, maximum, peak, the start and end times, the note and the calibration offset you used.

Information stored on your device

  • Noise logs. The start and end times, label, note, summary values and per-second level values of each log.
  • Calibration profiles. The profile name, the calibration offset, the phone model name and the time the profile was created.
  • Settings. Your choices, such as weighting, time response, the full-scale estimate, display options, the time until which a rewarded video has removed the banner, and whether an active subscription was found.

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for SoundCheck, so this data is not copied to your Google account backup. The app does not send this data to the developer.

Ads (Google AdMob)

SoundCheck shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on the home screen, and an optional rewarded video that removes the banner for 24 hours. AdMob may use the advertising ID of your device (the Android AD_ID permission) and information about the ad. Before any ad is requested in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, SoundCheck asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform, and it does not request ads unless the consent platform allows it. Ads are not shown while a noise log is recording. Google handles ad data under its own terms: Google privacy policy. You can change ad personalisation in your Android settings under Privacy > Ads.

Subscription (Google Play Billing)

An optional subscription removes the banner. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. SoundCheck receives only whether a subscription is active. It does not receive your payment details.

Internet

Measuring, logging, calibration, the widget and CSV export all work offline. The app uses the internet only for ads and for Google Play Billing.

Export and sharing

You can export a noise log as a CSV file. SoundCheck writes the file to its private cache and gives it to the Android share sheet, which you use to choose where it goes. What happens next depends on the app or service you choose. The CSV file contains the time, levels, weighting, calibration offset and a note that the measurement is not a certified meter.

Home-screen widget

The widget shows the summary of your most recent noise log, read from the database on your phone. It does not start the microphone, and it does not send anything.

Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO). Sound level measurement, as described above.
  • Foreground service and microphone service. Keeps a recording running with a notification while the app is in the background.
  • Notifications. Shows the recording notification.
  • Internet and network state. Ads and Google Play Billing only.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID). Used by Google AdMob, as described above.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data removes all noise logs, calibration profiles and settings from the app. Exported CSV files you shared are not controlled by SoundCheck. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your phone. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

SoundCheck is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
