AgeDay Privacy Policy

App: AgeDay (ageday) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

AgeDay is an age and date calculator for Android. All calculations happen on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Saved birthdays: the name or label you type and the birth date.
  • Saved countdowns: the label and event date.
  • Display settings such as the theme.

Birthdays and countdowns are kept in one JSON file and the settings in the app's private storage. These entries can be about other people, so enter only what you need. Android's automatic backup is turned off for AgeDay (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

What leaves your device

No files leave the app, and there is no share or export action. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Ads, accounts and purchases

AgeDay has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

AgeDay is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling AgeDay deletes every saved birthday and countdown with the app's private storage. You can also clear them from Android Settings > Apps > AgeDay > Storage > Clear storage. The app creates no copies outside its own storage, so nothing else is left behind.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
