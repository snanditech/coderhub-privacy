Paperplay Privacy Policy

App: Paperplay (com.coderhub.paperplay) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Paperplay is a noughts and crosses (tic-tac-toe) game. It has no accounts, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics. The app does not connect to the internet, and the developer does not run any server for Paperplay. Everything the app stores stays on your phone, and the developer receives none of it.

Information stored on your device

  • Settings. Your theme (follow system, light or dark), whether sound is on, whether vibration on each move is on, and your last choice of mode, difficulty, you-play-as mark and match length.
  • Game in progress. The match you are playing, so it can be resumed if Android closes the app. It is removed when the match ends.
  • Record. The running count of X wins, O wins and draws, and your record against each level of the computer, the matches you have won and the achievements you have earned.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. The app does not read your contacts, photos, files, location or any other app's data.

Network

Paperplay does not request the internet permission and sends nothing anywhere. The computer opponent runs on your phone.

Permissions

  • Vibrate. Lets the game give a short buzz on moves and results. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

The app asks for no other permissions.

Sound

Sound effects are generated inside the app and played through the phone's speaker. You can turn them off in Settings.

Backups

Android's automatic backup is turned off for Paperplay, so your settings, record and game in progress are not copied to your Google account or to a backup of your phone.

Data deletion

To delete everything Paperplay has stored, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android's app settings. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Paperplay is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
