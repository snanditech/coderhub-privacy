Nestly Privacy Policy

App: Nestly – Home Chores & Bills (com.coderhub.nestly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Nestly is a household admin app. It keeps chores, bills, a shopping list, document links with expiry dates, members and an activity log on your phone. The developer does not run a server for Nestly, does not have an account system, does not receive your household data, and does not sell or share it. Nestly has no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. It shows ads from Google AdMob, as described below.

Information stored on your device

  • Household data. Household name, invite code, member names, chores, bills and split amounts, shopping items, document titles and expiry dates, and the activity log are stored in a local database in the app's private storage.
  • Document links. When you add a document, you pick a file with Android's system file picker. Nestly stores the file's title and the link Android gives it. Nestly does not copy or upload the file. Opening a document uses the file where it already is on your device.
  • Settings. Theme, notification switch, ad consent state and subscription state are stored in the app's private settings storage.

Notifications

The optional daily summary is created on your phone by a scheduled background task (WorkManager). It counts overdue chores, bills and documents from the local data. On Android 13 and newer, Nestly asks for the notification permission when you turn the summary on. You can turn the summary off in Settings.

Backups you save

Settings > Backup writes the household to a JSON file in a location you choose with Android's file picker. The file is not encrypted, and it is not sent anywhere by Nestly. You can import the file later to restore the data on this phone. Anyone who has the file can read its contents, so store it somewhere private. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Nestly.

Ads

  • Banner. A small banner on the home screen is provided by Google AdMob.
  • Rewarded video. In Settings you can watch a short video to hide the banner for 24 hours. The video is provided by Google AdMob.
  • Consent. Before any ad loads, Nestly checks consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. Ads load only when that check allows them. Where it is required, Settings shows a Privacy options entry so you can change your choices.
  • Advertising ID. The app declares the Android advertising ID permission that AdMob uses. Google AdMob handles ad requests, and any data it collects is governed by its own policies: Google privacy policy.
  • Network. Ad requests are the only network requests Nestly makes, apart from the purchase check described next.

Subscription

Nestly offers an optional monthly subscription that hides the banner. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. Nestly receives only whether the subscription is active, and keeps that flag on your phone. Nestly does not receive your payment details.

Invite code

The invite code and its QR image are local labels for the household on your phone. Nestly does not connect devices or share household data between phones.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Needed to load ads and to check the subscription with Google Play.
  • Notifications. The optional daily summary.
  • Advertising ID. Used by Google AdMob for ads, as described above.

Nestly does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone-number access.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes the household, members, chores, bills, shopping items, document links and settings from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data. Backup files you saved, and the document files you linked, stay where you saved them. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us. Ad data held by Google is handled under Google's policies.

Children

Nestly is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
