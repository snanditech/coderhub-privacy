Unitly Privacy Policy

App: Unitly – Unit & Currency Converter (com.coderhub.unitly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Unitly converts units and currencies. Your history, favorites and settings stay on your phone. The developer does not run a server for Unitly, has no accounts, and does not collect personal information. Unitly shows a banner ad from Google AdMob on the home screen, and it can show a rewarded video ad that you choose to watch. Ads are described below.

Network requests

  • Exchange rates. Once a day, and when you tap Refresh rates, Unitly downloads the current currency reference rates from a public rate service (open.er-api.com, with frankfurter.dev as a backup). The request contains no personal data and no identifier from your phone. The service sees your IP address, as with any internet request.
  • Ads. Google AdMob loads the home-screen banner and any rewarded video. See the section on ads.
  • Subscription. If you buy the Pro subscription, Google Play handles the payment. The developer receives only the purchase status that Google Play reports, not your payment details.

Unit conversions run entirely on your phone and need no network. Currency conversions use the last rates saved on your phone, so they keep working offline with a "rates as of" date.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Banner. A small banner on the home screen, shown unless you have Pro or an active ad-free period.
  • Rewarded video. Optional. If you choose "Watch a video" in Settings, you see a video ad and get 24 hours without the banner.
  • Consent. Where the law requires it, Unitly asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before it loads any ad. If you do not consent, no ads are loaded. Ads are requested only after the consent check says ads are allowed.
  • Advertising ID. AdMob may use the Android advertising ID to show ads. Unitly does not link it to any other data it holds, because Unitly holds no personal data to link.

Google handles ad data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Information stored on your device

  • History and favorites. Your conversions (up to 50 in the free version) and your saved favorites are kept in the app's private database.
  • Rates. The last downloaded currency rates are kept in the same database.
  • Settings. Theme, number format, default category, rate refresh, and the ad-free period are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Home-screen widget. The widget stores which favorite it shows, on your phone only.

Unitly does not store your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Unitly, so your data is not uploaded to Google's backup service.

Permissions

  • Internet. Daily rate refresh, the ad banner and video ads, and Google Play purchases.
  • Network state. Lets Unitly skip rate refresh when there is no connection.
  • Advertising ID. Added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ads.

Unitly asks for no other permission.

Data deletion

Settings > Wipe all app data removes your history, favorites, saved rates and settings. A widget's choice is removed when you remove the widget. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Unitly is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads are served by Google, which applies its own rules for ads shown to children.

Changes
