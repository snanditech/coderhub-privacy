Housie Caller Privacy Policy

App: Housie Caller (housiecaller) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

Housie Caller is a Tambola/Housie number caller for family and party games. It draws numbers, speaks calls, prints tickets and checks claims on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • The current game's draw order, board and called numbers, so a restart resumes without repeats.
  • Generated tickets and their marked numbers.
  • Settings such as theme, call language, calling pace and whether voice is on.

These are JSON files and private preferences in the app's own storage. Android's automatic backup is turned off for Housie Caller (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

When you print tickets, an A4 PDF is written to the app's cache and shared read-only through a FileProvider URI. An app you send it to keeps its own copy under its own policy.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Speech and the microphone

Calls are spoken by the TextToSpeech engine installed on your device. Housie Caller sends no text to a server and declares no INTERNET permission, but speech is only as offline as your chosen engine and voice: some TTS engines can download voice data or use network voices depending on the engine, the installed language data and your Android text-to-speech settings. If speech must stay entirely offline, install and select an offline voice in Android's TTS settings, or turn voice off in Housie Caller.

The app does not record audio and does not request microphone access.

Ads, accounts and purchases

Housie Caller has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

Housie Caller is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Housie Caller deletes the current game, tickets and settings with the app's private storage. You can also remove them with Android Settings > Apps > Housie Caller > Storage > Clear storage. A PDF you share or print from another app is outside the app and remains until you delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
