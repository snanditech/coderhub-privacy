GSTCalc Privacy Policy

App: GSTCalc (gstcalc) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

GSTCalc is a GST calculator and HSN/SAC reference finder for shopkeepers and accountants. Calculations and the bundled reference list work on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Up to 50 saved calculations: amounts, rate, add/remove/reverse mode, supply type, rounding, quantity and the resulting tax figures.
  • Settings such as the theme and the last used calculator options.

History is one JSON file and settings are private preferences in the app's storage. Android's automatic backup is turned off for GSTCalc (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else. The 442-entry HSN/SAC list is bundled in the app; searches are not sent to a server.

When you export history, a CSV file is written to the app's cache and shared through Android's share sheet as a read-only FileProvider URI. The receiving app keeps its own copy under its own policy.

The Copy button places the tax break-up on Android's system clipboard. The clipboard is shared across apps, and clipboard managers or other apps may be able to read it; clear it if the figures are sensitive.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Ads, accounts and purchases

GSTCalc has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

GSTCalc is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling GSTCalc deletes the saved calculation history and settings with the app's private storage. You can also clear history in the app, or clear everything from Android Settings > Apps > GSTCalc > Storage > Clear storage. Shared CSV files are outside the app and remain until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
