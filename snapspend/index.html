SnapSpend Privacy Policy

App: SnapSpend – Receipt Scanner (com.coderhub.snapspend) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

SnapSpend is an expense app. You pick a photo of a receipt from your gallery, the text is read on your phone, and you check and save the expense. Receipts, receipt photos, budgets and settings are stored on your phone. The developer does not run a server for SnapSpend, does not receive your receipts or photos, and does not sell or share your data. SnapSpend has no accounts and no sign-in. It does show a small banner ad on the home screen, as described below.

Receipt photos and text recognition

  • On-device text reading. When you pick a receipt, SnapSpend reads the text with Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs on your phone. The receipt photo is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Photo copies. SnapSpend copies the picked photo into its private app storage on your phone and saves a compressed JPEG copy. The copy is shown with the expense and is deleted when you delete the expense or delete all data.
  • Photo access. SnapSpend uses Android's photo picker, so it gets access only to the photo you choose. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library.

Information stored on your device

  • Expenses: merchant, total, tax, currency, date, category, notes, and the lines read from the receipt.
  • Receipt photos (compressed copies) in the app's private storage.
  • Category budgets, merchant rules and settings (theme, default currency, ad-free status).
  • Export history, which records when you exported and how many rows were written.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. Android backup for SnapSpend is turned off, so this data is not copied to your Google account backup.

Ads (Google AdMob)

SnapSpend shows one small banner ad on the home screen, and offers a rewarded video that hides the banner for 24 hours. The ads are provided by Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested, SnapSpend asks Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) whether you have consented, and it requests ads only when UMP allows it. Where the law requires it, you can review or change your choices with the Privacy options button in Settings.

When ads are shown, Google may process your IP address, device identifiers and the advertising ID, as described in Google's privacy policy for its partners. The developer does not receive that information. The ads use test ad units until live ad units are set up, and this policy will be updated when they are.

Subscription (Google Play Billing)

An optional monthly subscription removes the home-screen banner. Purchases are handled by Google Play. SnapSpend receives only the subscription status from Google Play, not your payment details.

Internet use

SnapSpend uses the internet only for the ads, the consent check, and the subscription check described above. Reading receipts, searching, budgets, reports and exports all work without a network connection.

Exports

When you export, SnapSpend writes a CSV file to a location you choose with Android's file picker. The file is yours. What happens to it after that depends on where you save or share it.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Ads, consent and subscription checks described above.
  • Advertising ID. Used by Google AdMob for ads. Can be removed in your device's Google settings.

Accuracy

Text reading can make mistakes. SnapSpend marks which fields were read from the receipt and which need your check. Always check amounts before you use them for tax, accounting or a reimbursement claim.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes every receipt, receipt photo, budget, merchant rule and setting from the app. You can also delete a single expense from its detail screen. Exported CSV files are yours and stay where you saved them. Uninstalling the app removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

SnapSpend is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes
