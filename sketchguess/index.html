Sketchguess Privacy Policy

App: Sketchguess (sketchguess) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

Sketchguess is a pass-and-play drawing and guessing game for two to six players on one phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • The game in progress: players, scores, the current word, the drawing and whose turn it is.
  • Custom word lists that you create in the app.
  • The last 50 results, with the scoreboard.
  • Settings such as theme, seconds per turn and the number of rounds.

The active game, custom decks and history are JSON files in the app's private storage and settings are private preferences. Android's automatic backup is turned off for Sketchguess (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

Sketchguess does not add its own encryption to these records; they rely on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

The app has no share or export feature, so it creates no copies of your data outside its own storage.

Drawings are finger strokes stored as coordinates inside the saved game. They are not images in the gallery and are never posted anywhere. All players share the one phone, and there is no online room, chat or reporting channel.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android runtime permission. It does not ask for storage, camera, microphone, location, contacts or notification access.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's own signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Ads, accounts and purchases

Sketchguess has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

Sketchguess is not directed at children under 13. It is a game with no chat, no user-generated content feed and no way to reach other players, whether online or offline. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Sketchguess deletes its saved game, custom word lists, history and settings. You can also delete custom lists and clear history in the app, or clear everything from Android Settings > Apps > Sketchguess > Storage > Clear storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
