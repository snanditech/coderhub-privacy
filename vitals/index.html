Vitals Privacy Policy

App: Vitals – Battery Health Monitor (com.coderhub.vitals) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Vitals is a read-only monitor for your phone's battery and device. It reads what Android reports about the battery, charging, temperature, power modes and the device, and it stores that history on your phone. The developer does not run a server for Vitals, does not receive your battery readings or charge history, and does not sell or share your data. Vitals has no accounts and no analytics SDK. It shows ads through Google AdMob, as described below.

Information Vitals reads on your device

  • Battery. Level, status (charging, discharging, full), plug type, health flag, temperature, voltage, and where the phone reports them, current and charge counter.
  • Power and thermal state. Thermal status, battery saver, Doze and standby state.
  • Device details. Manufacturer, model, processor, supported CPU types, Android version, security patch date, screen size, memory and storage totals. Vitals never reads your files, contacts, messages, photos or installed apps.

Vitals does not request location, storage, contacts, microphone, camera, usage access or any special access.

Information stored on your device

  • Battery history. Readings taken about every 15 minutes in the background, and charge sessions (start and end time and level, and an estimate of charge added), stored in the app's private database. An estimated battery capacity is calculated from these sessions.
  • Settings. Units, refresh interval, data retention, reference capacity, alert choices and ad choices, stored in the app's private storage.
  • History is kept for the number of days you choose in Settings (7, 30 or 90 days). Older entries are deleted automatically.

Vitals does not send this history anywhere. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Vitals, so the history is not copied to your Google account backup.

Notifications

If you turn on alerts (charge limit reminder, full charge, high temperature, low battery), Vitals posts them on your phone. Notifications are created from the readings on your phone and are not sent to any server. You can turn them off in Settings or in Android's notification settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Vitals shows a banner on its home screen and offers an optional rewarded video that removes the banner for 24 hours. These ads are served by Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested, Vitals asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform where the law requires it. Ads are requested only when that consent allows it. If you are in a region where consent is required, Settings > Privacy options lets you change your choices.

AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and other technical data (such as IP address, device model and app use) to show and measure ads, under Google's privacy policy. The developer does not receive this data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Subscription (Google Play Billing)

The optional subscription that removes the banner is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and the account. Vitals receives only whether the subscription is active, and it re-checks this with Google Play when it starts.

Network use

Vitals uses the internet only for the ads and the subscription check described above. Every battery reading, chart and estimate works fully offline.

Background work

Vitals uses Android's WorkManager to take a battery reading about every 15 minutes. This work runs only on your phone, and Android sets the schedule. Vitals does not use a foreground service, a persistent notification or a wake lock.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes the battery history, charge sessions and settings from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Vitals is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
