Rounds Privacy Policy

App: Rounds – Interval & HIIT Timer (com.coderhub.rounds) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Rounds is an interval and HIIT timer. Your workouts and your workout history are stored on your phone. The developer does not run any server for Rounds, does not have accounts, and does not receive your workouts or history. Rounds has no analytics SDK. The only services that receive data are the advertising and purchase services described below, and only for the purposes described there.

Workouts and history on your device

  • Workouts. The presets and the custom workouts you create (names, work, rest, round, set and cool-down times) are stored in the app's private database on your phone.
  • History. Each finished or stopped session is stored on your phone: the workout name, start and end time, rounds completed and time worked and rested. The History screen uses this to show streaks and totals.
  • Settings. Sound, vibration, voice, theme and keep-screen-on choices, and the state of your ad-free period or subscription, are stored in the app's private storage.
  • Sharing and importing. When you share a workout, Rounds opens Android's share sheet with the workout as plain text. The app you pick receives that text. When you import a file, Rounds reads only the file you choose, and saves the valid workouts to your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

Timer, sound, vibration and voice

  • Background timing. While a workout is running, Rounds runs a foreground service with an ongoing notification so that the timer and its cues keep working with the screen off. A partial wake lock keeps the processor awake only while a workout is running. Both stop when the workout ends or is paused.
  • Notifications. Rounds asks for permission to post notifications, so that the notification with the timer and its Pause, Resume and Stop buttons can be shown. The notification is not used for advertising.
  • Voice cues (optional, off by default). When voice cues are on, Rounds gives the phrases (for example "Work" or "Rest") to the text-to-speech engine on your phone. Rounds does not record your voice; it has no microphone access.
  • Vibration. Rounds uses the vibration motor for cues when vibration is turned on.
  • Keep screen on (optional, off by default). Keeps the screen lit while a workout is shown.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Rounds shows a small banner ad on the home screen, and offers a rewarded video that turns the banner off for 24 hours. The ads are served by Google AdMob. Ads are not shown during a workout or on the session, history or settings screens.

  • Consent. Before any ad is requested, Rounds uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show a consent form where it is required, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. Rounds requests ads only when UMP reports that ads may be requested. If the consent check fails, no ads are requested.
  • What Google receives. To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK receives technical information such as your IP address, device and app information, and the advertising ID that Android provides, unless you have turned ad personalisation off in Android settings. Google uses this as described in its privacy policy for Google partners.
  • Privacy options. In Settings, the Privacy options entry is shown when UMP says you can change your choices. It opens Google's consent form so you can change them.

Subscription (Google Play Billing)

An optional monthly subscription removes the home banner. Purchases are handled by Google Play. Rounds receives from Google Play only whether your subscription is active, and it keeps that status on your phone. It does not receive your payment details or name. Rounds checks the subscription with Google Play when it starts, so a cancelled subscription ends the benefit.

Network use

Rounds needs the internet only for ads, the consent form, and the subscription check. Every timer, history, streak, share and import feature works offline, and the timer does not depend on a network connection.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Ads, the consent form and the subscription check described above.
  • Advertising ID. Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads, as described above.
  • Foreground service and special use. Keeps the workout timer running with the screen off.
  • Notifications. Shows the timer notification and its controls.
  • Wake lock. Keeps the timer running with the screen off, only while a workout runs.
  • Vibrate. Haptic workout cues.

Rounds does not ask for location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or body sensors.

Children

Rounds is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes your workouts, history and settings from the app. Uninstalling Rounds removes its private data from your phone. Android's automatic app backup is turned off for Rounds, so your data is not copied to Google Drive. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us. Google may hold its own records of ads and purchases under its own policies.
