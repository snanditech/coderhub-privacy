TipSplit Privacy Policy

App: TipSplit – Tip & Bill Splitter (com.coderhub.tipsplit) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

TipSplit is a bill splitter. You enter a bill, a tip, tax and service charges, and the people who share it. The app works offline and keeps your bills on your phone. The developer does not run any server for TipSplit, does not receive your bills, receipts or people's names, and does not sell or share your data. TipSplit has no account, no contacts access, no location, no camera permission and no analytics SDK.

What stays on your device

  • Bills. Amounts, tip and tax settings, rounding choices, people's names, items and who paid are stored in the app's private database on your phone.
  • Receipt photos. If you attach a receipt, you pick the photo with Android's photo picker. TipSplit copies it into the app's private storage, makes it smaller, and keeps it on your phone. Receipt photos are never uploaded.
  • Saved people. Names you used on recent bills are kept on your phone so you can add them quickly.
  • Settings. Default tip, currency, rounding, theme and similar choices are kept in the app's private storage.

Settle-up is a record only. TipSplit works out who owes whom and records whether it was settled. It never moves money and has no bank or payment connection.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a small banner on the home screen and offers an optional rewarded video that turns the banner off for 24 hours. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, the app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may read the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and send your IP address and device details to Google.

  • Consent first. Before any ad loads, the app asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). If consent is not given, the app does not load ads.
  • Privacy options. In Settings, the "Privacy options" entry appears where Google requires it and lets you change your ad consent at any time.
  • Google's terms. Google handles ad requests under its own policies: Google privacy policy.

Ads never appear while you enter a bill or on the settle-up summary.

Pro subscription (Google Play Billing)

The optional Pro subscription removes the banner. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The app receives only whether the subscription is active. TipSplit does not see your payment details.

Internet access

TipSplit uses the internet only for ads, consent and the Pro subscription described above. Bills, receipts and people's names are never sent over the network.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Ads, consent and Play Billing.
  • Advertising ID. Used by Google AdMob, as described above.

TipSplit does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, notification or call permissions.

Backups

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for TipSplit, so your bills are not copied to your Google account backup.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data removes every bill, participant, settlement, receipt copy, saved person and setting from the app. In History, Clear removes all saved bills and their receipt copies. Deleting a single bill also removes its receipt copy. Uninstalling the app removes all of its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

TipSplit is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
