Lumen Privacy Policy

App: Lumen: Research Paper Reader (com.coderhub.lumen) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Lumen is a reader for research papers and web articles. You import a PDF, a web page or pasted text, and Lumen keeps it on your phone so you can read, search, take notes, make flashcards and copy citations. Lumen has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics SDK, and the developer does not run a server that receives your papers, notes or flashcards. Lumen shows ads from Google AdMob, and it can send text to an AI provider only when you set one up yourself and use an AI feature.

Papers, notes and flashcards

  • Import. You can open a PDF with Android's file picker, paste text, enter a web address, or share a PDF, link or text from another app to Lumen. PDF text is extracted on your phone. Lumen does not upload your files.
  • Stored on your device. Imported text, page splits, notes, flashcards and review progress are kept in Lumen's private database on your phone. Settings, theme, text size, read-aloud speed and citation details are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Offline reading. Once a paper is imported, reading, search, notes, flashcards, read-aloud and citations work without an internet connection.

Web pages

When you enter or share a web address, Lumen downloads only that one page, to read its text. It does not crawl other pages or follow links to collect more. Lumen does not check a site's robots.txt file, because it makes one request at your direction, as a browser does. Lumen refuses pages larger than 5 MB. Your web request reaches the site you chose, which sees your IP address and the usual request details under its own privacy policy.

Read-aloud

Read-aloud uses the text-to-speech engine on your device. The text is not sent anywhere.

AI features (optional, bring your own key)

  • Off by default. Summaries, questions and explanations need an OpenAI-compatible provider that you set up in Settings: a base URL, your own API key and a model. Without these, the AI features stay off.
  • What is sent. When you use an AI feature, Lumen sends the text you chose to that provider: a whole paper for a summary, relevant passages with your question, or the passage you selected for an explanation. Lumen shows which provider receives the text. The provider handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Your key. The API key is encrypted on your phone with a key held in the Android Keystore. Lumen does not send your key to the developer.
  • AI output. AI answers are labelled as AI-generated and may be wrong. The Report this result button opens an email to the developer with the result and the provider's host name. Lumen sends nothing until you send that email.

Ads and Pro

  • Google AdMob. Lumen shows a banner on the library screen. The ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID and device information under Google's policies. You can watch a short rewarded video to hide the banner for 24 hours, or subscribe to Lumen Pro to hide it.
  • Consent. Lumen uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where required by law. Lumen requests ads only when that consent allows it. In Settings, Privacy options appears when required, so you can change your choice.
  • Subscriptions. Lumen Pro is handled by Google Play Billing. Lumen receives only whether a subscription is active. It never sees your payment details.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Fetching a web page you chose, the optional AI provider, ads and Play Billing.
  • Advertising ID. Used by Google AdMob for ads.

Lumen does not ask for location, contacts, the camera, the microphone or storage access beyond the files you pick.

Backups

Android's automatic device backup is turned off for Lumen, so your papers, notes and settings are not copied to Google's backup service.

Copyright and sharing

Lumen keeps the text of papers and web pages for your personal reading and study. Exports contain your notes, your quotes and your flashcards, and quotes in notes are short. Lumen does not publish or redistribute source text. Please respect the copyright of the material you import.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes your papers, notes, flashcards, citation details and settings from the app. Uninstalling Lumen also removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Lumen is not designed for children under 13. Its ads are served by Google AdMob, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
