EMIWise Privacy Policy

App: EMIWise (emiwise) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

EMIWise is a loan and investment calculator for India. All arithmetic runs on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no lending marketplace, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

EMIWise stores only your theme choice. The numbers you type into a calculator are held while the screen is open and are not saved when you leave it; the app keeps no calculation history.

The theme setting is in the app's private SharedPreferences. Android's automatic backup is turned off for EMIWise (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy it to Google Drive.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

When you export a result, the PDF is built on the device, written to the app's cache and shared read-only through a FileProvider URI. Results can also be shared as plain text. The PDF is temporary app cache, but an app you share it to keeps its own copy under its own policy. There is no save-to-folder action for PDFs in this version.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Calculations and accuracy

EMI, prepayment, loan comparison, SIP, lump sum, fixed deposit, recurring deposit, PPF, GST and income-tax arithmetic all run in the app. Nothing is sent to a bank, lender or tax service, and there are no offers, lead forms or lender links.

Results are estimates for planning. Rates, bank rules and tax law change; confirm figures with your bank or a tax professional before acting on them.

Ads, accounts and purchases

EMIWise has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

EMIWise is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling EMIWise removes the theme setting and any PDFs left in the app cache. There is no saved calculation history to delete. Copies you shared with another app remain with that app; delete them there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
