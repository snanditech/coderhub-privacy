Headsay Privacy Policy

App: Headsay (headsay) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

Headsay is a one-phone forehead-guessing party game for 2–6 teams. The game runs entirely on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no online play, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Custom decks you type or import: the deck name, description and card text.
  • The match in progress, including team names you enter and the current scores.
  • History of the last 50 games, including team names, scores, best turn and decks used.
  • Settings such as theme, turn length, pass penalty, tilt controls and haptics.

These are JSON files and private preferences in the app's storage. Android's automatic backup is turned off for Headsay (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

You can share a finished result as plain text, or share a custom deck as JSON text, through Android's share sheet. Those actions create a copy in the app you choose; that app handles it under its own policy. The shared copy remains available to the receiving app after you uninstall Headsay.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Ads, accounts and purchases

Headsay has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

Headsay is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Headsay deletes custom decks, the saved match, game history and settings with the app's private storage. You can also delete custom decks and history inside the app, or clear everything from Android Settings > Apps > Headsay > Storage > Clear storage. Shared result text and deck JSON are outside the app and remain until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
