Jadely Privacy Policy

App: Jadely (com.coderhub.jadely) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Jadely is a single-player mahjong solitaire game. It has no accounts, no network features, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics. The app does not connect to the internet and does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything the game keeps (your settings, the game in progress, your results and achievements) stays on your phone. The developer runs no server for Jadely.

What the app stores on your device

  • Settings. Your theme choice (follow system, light or dark), and whether sound and vibration are on.
  • The game in progress. The board, the moves and the hints and shuffles left, so Continue can resume it after the app is closed. It is removed when the board is cleared.
  • Results. Counts of boards cleared and stuck per layout, your win streaks, clean clears (no hint and no shuffle used) and which local achievements you have earned.

These are kept in the app's private storage. You can clear the results at any time from Stats, Reset stats. Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

Backups

Android's automatic backup is turned off for Jadely, so your settings, saved game and results are not copied to Google Drive or to another device through cloud backup.

Permissions

Jadely asks for no permissions. It does not use the internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage.

  • Sound and vibration. Short tones and vibration pulses on moves and results. They are optional and can be turned off in Settings. Vibration uses the system haptic feedback, which does not need a permission.

Advertising and analytics

Jadely shows no advertising and includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Nothing about your play is sent to the developer or any third party.

Children

Jadely is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data deletion

Stats, Reset stats clears your results. Uninstalling the app removes all data the app keeps on your phone. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
