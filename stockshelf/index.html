StockShelf Privacy Policy

App: StockShelf (stockshelf) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

StockShelf is an offline stock ledger for a small shop or home business: items, stock movements, costs, low-stock levels and CSV/PDF reports. It has no account, no developer server and no INTERNET permission. It is a record of your entries, not an audited valuation or accounting/tax advice.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps stockshelf.json in private storage, with a local .bak copy and quarantined corrupt files. It contains items (name, SKU, barcode, category, unit, quantity, average cost, sale price, reorder level, active flag and timestamps) and movements (item id and item-name snapshot, type, signed quantity change, unit cost, note and timestamp). Private preferences hold the theme and currency symbol. No customer or supplier records are stored.

What leaves the device

Nothing is uploaded to the developer. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no developer server, advertising or analytics code.

Item CSV, movement CSV and PDF reports leave only when you choose a destination in the system file picker. They contain the item names, quantities, costs, prices and movement notes. The storage provider or app you choose keeps its own copy under its own policy and may use a network. The app has no share-sheet export.

Permissions

The app declares no Android runtime permission. It does not request storage, camera, location, microphone, contacts or notification access. Reports use the Storage Access Framework, so no storage permission is needed.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Android backup and device transfer

Android automatic backup is turned off for this app (the manifest sets allowBackup=false and fullBackupContent=false). Its current extraction rules exclude only the database and sharedpref domains from cloud backup; the app's files/ records are not named in a separate rule. The file also has no device-transfer block. The manifest switch is the primary control, and the extraction XML should be widened if backup is ever enabled.

Inventory and valuation limits

StockShelf uses whole-number quantities and integer paise with an explicit weighted-average cost; it does not provide fractional weights or volumes. The report is a record of the entries you typed, not an audited stock valuation, tax document or accounting advice. A positive adjustment adds units without a purchase cost, so an item with a zero/unknown average keeps a zero stock value until a stock-in supplies a cost. Deleting an item permanently deletes its movements; archive is the history-preserving option. Barcode entry is manual; no camera is used and only EAN-13 can be drawn.

Ads, accounts and purchases

The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no advertising, analytics or billing SDK. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

StockShelf is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Delete an item in the app to delete it and its movements, or use Reset all in Settings. Clearing the app's storage from Android Settings > Apps > StockShelf > Storage also deletes the records. Uninstalling the app deletes its private storage. CSV/PDF files you saved are outside the app and remain until you delete them. There is no developer server copy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
