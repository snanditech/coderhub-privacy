Pixmend Privacy Policy

App: Pixmend – AI Photo Fixer (com.coderhub.pixmend) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Pixmend is a photo editor. Colour edits, scene labelling and auto enhance run on your phone. The developer does not run any server for Pixmend and does not receive your photos. Pixmend has no accounts and no analytics SDK. It shows ads (Google AdMob) and offers an optional Pro subscription that removes the home screen banner. The only network use is the optional cloud enhance, which sends a photo to an endpoint you configure yourself, and the ads and subscription services described below.

Photos

  • Picking photos. You choose photos with Android's photo picker. Pixmend gets access only to the photos you pick and does not read your gallery.
  • Your originals are not changed. When you pick a photo, Pixmend makes a copy in its private storage and edits that copy. Your original stays as it was.
  • Saved copies. When you save a result, Pixmend writes a new JPEG to Pictures/Pixmend in your gallery (Android 10 and newer). On older Android versions you choose where the file goes. Saved copies do not contain the original file's location or capture-time metadata.
  • Working files. Source copies and rendered results are kept in the app's private storage until you delete the project or all data.

On-device processing

Brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, auto enhance, rotation, batch presets and scene labelling (Google ML Kit image labelling, run on the device) all work without a network connection. Scene labels stay on your phone and are shown only in the editor.

Cloud enhance (optional, your own endpoint)

  • Cloud enhance is off until you add a base URL and API key in Settings. Pixmend ships with no key and no server of its own.
  • When you run cloud enhance, Pixmend sends the photo, the model name you entered and your key to the base URL you entered. The base URL must use https. Pixmend sends nothing to any other cloud service for this feature.
  • Your API key is stored on your phone, encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore. It is not included in Android backups.
  • Results from cloud enhance are AI-generated and may be inaccurate. Pixmend labels them as AI-generated. You can report a result from the Result screen; that opens an email to the developer with the project number only. No photo is attached.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Pixmend shows a small banner on the home screen and offers a rewarded video that removes the banner for 24 hours.
  • Ads are requested only after the consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) allows it. Where UMP says ads cannot be requested, no ads load.
  • Google AdMob receives technical data needed to show ads, such as the advertising ID (Android permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), subject to your consent choices. Pixmend does not send your photos to ads.
  • If UMP requires it, Settings > Ads and privacy has a Privacy options button where you can change your choices.
  • Google's policies: Google privacy policy and how Google uses data from ads.

Pro subscription and purchases

The Pro subscription is handled by Google Play Billing. Pixmend receives only whether your subscription is active. It removes the home screen banner. Google Play handles payment and holds your purchase details. Pixmend does not see your payment information.

Internet permission

Pixmend requests internet access for the optional cloud enhance, the ads, and the subscription check. Colour edits, auto enhance, batch presets, rotation and scene labelling do not use the internet.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Cloud enhance to your endpoint, ads and subscription checks described above.
  • Advertising ID. Used by Google AdMob to show ads, subject to your consent choices.

Pixmend does not ask for camera, location, contacts, microphone or broad storage access. It does not use Android's cloud backup for your data.

Data deletion

Deleting a project in History removes its working copies from the app's private storage. Settings > Delete all data removes all projects, working copies, your API key and settings. Photos saved to your gallery are yours and stay there; delete them in your gallery app. Uninstalling the app removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Pixmend is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
