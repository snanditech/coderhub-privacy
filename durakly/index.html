Durakly Privacy Policy

App: Durakly (durakly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

Durakly is an offline individual podkidnoy durak game for two to six players, with optional perevodnoy transfers and on-device bots. It has no account, no developer server and no INTERNET permission. There are no online or Wi-Fi rooms, ads, in-app purchases or leaderboards.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • files/durakly/game.json and a previous-good copy game.bak: the current or most recent deal, including player count, transfer and first-bout options, seed/RNG state, dealer and round, every player's hand, talon, trump card and suit, discard, table with attacks and defences, attacker/defender/throw-in roles, limits, phase, bout number, durak result and the last event. It does not contain player names, contacts or an account identifier.
  • files/durakly/stats.json: games, escapes, times as durak, draws, current and best escape streak, and fields for cards beaten and pickups. The current gameplay flow records the first group; the beaten-card and pickup fields are present but are not updated by the current UI.
  • Private preferences in the app's settings SharedPreferences: theme, number of players, transfer rule, five-card first-bout switch, pass-and-play, bot level, large-card mode and fast-bot mode.

Writes use a temporary file and rename. A previous good deal is kept as game.bak; an interrupted write can leave a .tmp file until it is overwritten or app storage is cleared. The app does not add its own encryption; these files rely on Android's app sandbox and device storage encryption.

What leaves the device

The app itself has no upload path and no network permission. There is no developer server, advertising, analytics, telemetry or billing code, and no user account.

There is no share sheet, export, print, cloud-sync or backup/restore feature in the source. Saved deals, statistics and preferences are not sent to CoderHub26. A copy outside the app's private files could come from Android or an original-equipment-manufacturer backup/transfer mechanism, described below, or from someone with access to the unlocked device or the app's data.

Permissions

The app declares no Android runtime permission. It does not request storage, camera, microphone, location, contacts, notification or network access. The only exported component is the launcher activity.

Like most AndroidX apps, the merged manifest can also contain the library's signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Android backup and device transfer

Android automatic backup is turned off in the manifest (allowBackup=false and fullBackupContent=false). The app's data_extraction_rules.xml currently excludes only the database and sharedpref domains from cloud backup. It does not name the app's files/ JSON records and has no separate device-transfer block. The manifest switch is the primary control, and the extraction rules are narrower than a blanket exclusion.

This page does not claim that device-to-device transfer or an OEM backup is impossible. There is no developer server copy to delete, and there is no app-level encryption beyond the Android app sandbox and device storage encryption.

Pass-and-play and local concealment

In pass-and-play, the acting player's hand is hidden behind a hand-off screen until that player taps to reveal it. This is local concealment on one shared phone. It is not user authentication, a PIN, biometrics, a separate user profile or cryptographic protection, and it does not stop someone who has the unlocked phone or access to the app's private storage from reading the saved deal. Statistics are not recorded for pass-and-play games in this version.

Rules, variants and limits

Durakly implements individual podkidnoy durak for two to six players, with optional perevodnoy transfer and the optional five-card cap for the first bout. It is a single-deal game with rematch; it does not implement partnership durak or a multi-deal series. Attack cards are played one at a time in this app rather than in a batch. On a pickup, the defender takes only the cards on the table; the optional extra attacker donation and the trump-six exchange are not included.

With six players all 36 cards are dealt and the last card dealt sets trumps. Refills follow the documented original-attacker order, with the transfer exception described in the help screen. The interface is English-only; Russian and Ukrainian localisation are not included. This is one documented podkidnoy/perevodnoy implementation, not a full referee for every regional durak variant.

Ads, accounts, purchases and gambling

There are no user accounts or sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no coins, no real-money play and no betting or wagering feature. The app does not use advertising, analytics, telemetry or billing SDKs. CoderHub26 does not collect, receive or sell data from the app.

Children

Durakly is a card game and is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Retention and deletion

Starting a new game replaces the current saved deal; the previous good copy can remain as game.bak until it is overwritten. There is no in-app statistics-reset button in this version, so statistics and preferences remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

To remove all app data, use Android Settings > Apps > Durakly > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall Durakly. That removes the private deal, backup, statistics and preference files. There is no exported or developer-server copy to delete because the app has no export or network feature.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
