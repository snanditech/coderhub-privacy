PixSqueeze Privacy Policy

App: PixSqueeze (pixsqueeze) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

PixSqueeze is a batch image compressor and converter for Android. Photos are decoded, resized and re-encoded on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Your last-used compression settings: mode, target size, quality, scale, pixel box, output format and the two switches.
  • Temporary working files in the app cache while a batch is being prepared, shared or saved. Cached outputs are deleted when you start a new batch or when Android reclaims cache space.

There is no history of photos: PixSqueeze does not keep a list of the pictures you opened, their names or their contents. Android's automatic backup is turned off for PixSqueeze (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy these settings to Google Drive.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

Photos are opened through the system photo picker, which grants read access only to the files you select. Your originals are not modified.

You can share one compressed file or a ZIP through Android's share sheet, or save the batch to a folder you choose with the system folder picker. Files saved to a folder are outside the app and remain after you uninstall PixSqueeze until you delete them. Apps you share to keep their own copies under their own policies.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Photo and folder access come from the system pickers and cover only the items you select.

Photo metadata

Strip metadata is on by default. Re-encoding then drops EXIF data, including GPS location.

If you turn the switch off, JPEG output keeps tags copied from the source such as capture date, camera make and model, exposure details and GPS location. PNG and WebP output stays clean. Check this before sharing a photo whose location you do not want to reveal.

Ads, accounts and purchases

PixSqueeze has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

PixSqueeze is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling PixSqueeze removes its settings and cached working files. Photos you saved to a folder, and copies you shared with another app, are outside the app and are not deleted; remove them where you saved them or in the receiving app. Your original photos are untouched.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
