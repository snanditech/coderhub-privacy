Klondly Privacy Policy

App: Klondly (com.coderhub.klondly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 17 November 2026

Summary

Klondly is a Klondike solitaire card game. The game itself works fully offline. The developer does not run any server for Klondly, does not receive any of your game data, and does not sell or share data. Klondly has no accounts, no in-app purchases and no analytics of its own. It shows ads from Google AdMob, using Google's ad SDK, and uses Google's User Messaging Platform for consent.

Network access and ads (Google AdMob)

Klondly shows a small banner ad on the menu, statistics and win screens. It never shows ads over the card table while you play. The banner is served by Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Nothing about your games, scores, statistics or saved game is sent to the developer or to Google as part of playing.

  • Consent. Before any ad is requested, Klondly uses Google's User Messaging Platform to show a consent form where one is required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). If consent is not given, or is not yet known, no ads are requested. You can change your choice later with the "Privacy options" button in Settings, when it is offered.
  • Information the ad SDK may collect. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device information and app-interaction data. This is used to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and for analytics, as described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. The developer does not read or store your advertising ID.
  • Permissions for ads. Klondly requests the internet and network-state permissions so that the banner can load, and the advertising ID permission that AdMob uses.

Information stored on your device

Klondly keeps the following in its private storage on your phone. The game progress is not sent to anyone:

  • Your settings: theme, sound on or off, vibration on or off, the chosen drawing mode, the card back and the table style.
  • Your best score for each drawing mode.
  • Your game statistics: games played and won, win streaks, your fastest win, and the daily deal streak.
  • The game in progress, so that Continue can pick it up after the app is closed. It is saved after every move.

The daily deal is generated on your phone from the date. No file is downloaded for it.

Permissions

  • Vibrate. Used for the short buzz on moves and for the win. You can turn vibration off in Settings. This permission is granted when the app is installed and does not ask you anything.

Klondly asks for no other permission: no location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files. The internet, network-state and advertising ID permissions are used only for the ad banner described above.

Backup

Klondly is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup, so your settings, statistics and game in progress stay on the phone they were played on.

Sharing

Klondly does not share your game data with anyone. The only third party involved is Google, through the ad SDK described above. It has no share button and no social features.

Data deletion

Use Settings > Apps > Klondly > Storage > Clear storage to erase your settings, statistics and saved game. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Klondly is made for players aged 13 and older. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The game itself collects none; the ad SDK described above may process the technical data listed there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
