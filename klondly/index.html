Klondly Privacy Policy

App: Klondly (com.coderhub.klondly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Klondly is a Klondike solitaire card game. It works fully offline. The developer does not run any server for Klondly, does not receive any of your game data, and does not sell or share data. Klondly has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Network access

Klondly does not request the internet permission and does not connect to the internet. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere.

Information stored on your device

Klondly keeps the following in its private storage on your phone, and nowhere else:

  • Your settings: theme, sound on or off, vibration on or off, the chosen drawing mode, the card back and the table style.
  • Your best score for each drawing mode.
  • Your game statistics: games played and won, win streaks, your fastest win, and the daily deal streak.
  • The game in progress, so that Continue can pick it up after the app is closed. It is saved after every move.

The daily deal is generated on your phone from the date. No file is downloaded for it.

Permissions

  • Vibrate. Used for the short buzz on moves and for the win. You can turn vibration off in Settings. This permission is granted when the app is installed and does not ask you anything.

Klondly asks for no other permission: no location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Backup

Klondly is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup, so your settings, statistics and game in progress stay on the phone they were played on.

Sharing

Klondly does not share anything with anyone. It has no share button and no social features.

Data deletion

Use Settings > Apps > Klondly > Storage > Clear storage to erase your settings, statistics and saved game. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Klondly is made for players aged 13 and older. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and it collects none at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
