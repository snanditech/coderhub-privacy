StackPal Privacy Policy

App: StackPal – Dev Tools & Errors (com.coderhub.stackpal) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

StackPal is a set of developer tools: JSON, JWT, Base64, URL, hashes, timestamps, regex, cron, colours, UUIDs, text diffs and an offline error explainer. The tools run on your phone. You do not need an account. The developer does not run a server for StackPal, does not receive what you type into the tools, and does not sell or share your data. StackPal has no analytics SDK.

What stays on your device

  • Tool input and output. Everything you paste into a tool, the error explainer included, is processed on your phone and is not sent anywhere.
  • Tool history. The recent runs of the code tools are stored in the app's private database so you can look them up. Known secrets are replaced with a placeholder before anything is saved, and the JWT decoder is never saved to history. You can clear the history in the History screen, or delete everything in Settings.
  • Settings. Your theme choice, your consent choice and your ad-free status are stored in the app's private settings storage.

Ads

StackPal shows one small banner ad on the home screen. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Before any ad is requested, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for consent where the law requires it. No ad is requested until UMP allows it. If you are in a region that requires it, Settings shows a Privacy options entry where you can change your choice.

  • Rewarded video. You can choose to watch a video to hide the banner for 24 hours. Google's ad SDK handles the video.
  • Advertising ID. The Google ad SDK can read the Android advertising ID so that ads can be shown and measured. Google's policies apply to that data: Google privacy policy.
  • Network requests. Ads and the consent step send requests to Google over the internet. These are the only network uses of StackPal apart from the subscription check below.

Subscription

An optional monthly subscription hides the banner ad. Payments are handled by Google Play Billing, and the developer never sees your payment details. StackPal asks Google Play whether the subscription is active, so that a cancelled subscription ends the ad-free status.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Used only to show ads, run the consent step and check the subscription. The tools never use the internet.
  • Advertising ID. Used by the Google ad SDK, as described above.

StackPal does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or notification permissions.

Backup

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for StackPal, so your history and settings are not copied to your Google account. Uninstalling the app removes its private data.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes the tool history and the app's settings from your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes this data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us. Google may keep records of ad requests under its own policies.

Children

StackPal is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
