Fluento Privacy Policy

App: Fluento – AI Conversation Coach (com.coderhub.fluento) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Fluento is a language practice app. You pick an everyday scenario, read or hear a partner's line, and reply by choosing an answer, typing or speaking. Your practice history, saved phrases, streaks and settings are stored on your phone. The developer does not run a server for Fluento, has no accounts, does not receive your conversations, and does not sell or share your data. Fluento has no analytics SDK.

Fluento shows ads from Google AdMob. Ad personalisation and consent are handled by Google's User Messaging Platform (see Ads below). Fluento uses the internet for ads, for the optional Pro subscription check, and, only if you add your own AI endpoint, to talk to that endpoint.

What stays on your device

  • Practice history. Sessions, the replies you chose or typed, scores, XP, streaks and your daily goal are kept in Fluento's private database on your phone.
  • Saved phrases and review. Phrases you save, their notes, and their review schedule are kept on your phone.
  • Settings. Theme, daily goal and ad and subscription status are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Your AI key. If you add one, the key is encrypted with a key stored in the Android Keystore and never leaves your phone except in the requests you send to your own endpoint.

Fluento does not save your microphone audio. Your speech is turned into text only while you use speech practice, and the text is treated like anything else you type.

Microphone and speech

  • When it is used. Fluento asks for the microphone permission the first time you tap Say it. The microphone is used only for that one phrase, and only while you tap the button.
  • Who turns speech into text. Fluento uses Android's built-in speech recognizer. Depending on your phone and its settings, the recognizer may process audio on the device or send it to Google's speech service under Google's terms. Fluento receives only the recognized text.
  • Spoken replies. Partner lines and reference replies are read aloud with Android's text-to-speech engine. The text is not sent anywhere by Fluento.
  • You can practise without the microphone by typing or choosing replies.

AI coach (optional, your own endpoint)

The open-ended AI coach is off until you enter a base URL and an API key in Settings. Fluento ships with no key and no AI provider. When you use the AI coach, Fluento sends the scenario name, the conversation so far and the text you typed to the endpoint you entered, over https only. Fluento shows the endpoint's host name in the practice screen. The endpoint's own operator handles that data under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive it.

Replies from the AI coach are generated by a model and may be wrong. You can report a reply from the practice screen. Report opens an email to the developer with the reply, your text and the endpoint's host name, and nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Fluento shows a small banner on the home screen and an optional rewarded video (watch it to turn off the banner for 24 hours).
  • Ads are requested only when Google's User Messaging Platform reports that ads may be requested. Where the law requires it, you see a consent form the first time you open the app. You can change your choice later with Privacy options in Settings, which appears in regions where it is required.
  • Google may collect device and ad identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, under Google's Privacy Policy. Fluento sends no conversation text or practice data to Google's ad services.
  • The Android advertising ID permission (AD_ID) is declared for the ads SDK.

Subscription (optional)

The optional Pro subscription removes the banner. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. Fluento receives only whether a subscription is active. Google Play handles payment and billing details under its own terms.

Internet and permissions

  • Internet. Used for ads, Google Play Billing, and your own AI endpoint if you set one up.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO). Only for speech practice, when you tap Say it.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID). Declared for Google's ads SDK.

Fluento does not request location, contacts, photos, files, camera or notification access. Android backup of Fluento's data is turned off, so your data is not copied to your Google account backup.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes your practice history, saved phrases, review progress, settings and AI key from the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Fluento is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Learning claims

Level and pronunciation figures in Fluento are estimates. They are not certified language levels or accent assessments.
