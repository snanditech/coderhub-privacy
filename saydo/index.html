Saydo Privacy Policy

App: Saydo: Voice to Tasks & Notes (com.coderhub.saydo) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Saydo records your voice, turns the transcript into tasks, and keeps them on your phone. The developer does not run a server for Saydo, does not receive your recordings, transcripts or tasks, and does not sell or share your data. Saydo has no accounts and no analytics. Saydo shows a banner ad on the home screen, which Google serves; see Ads below.

Microphone and speech recognition

  • When the microphone is used. Saydo asks for microphone access the first time you tap Record, or when you use the home screen widget or the Quick Settings tile. The microphone is used only while the red recording screen is open. A notification shows while recording, with a Stop button.
  • Speech recognition. Saydo uses the speech recognizer built into your phone (Android's SpeechRecognizer). It asks for offline recognition first. If the offline language pack is not installed on your phone, Android's speech service may send the audio to Google to be transcribed. Google handles that request under its own privacy policy. Saydo cannot control this step, but it tells you on the recording screen and in Settings whether your phone can recognize speech on the device.
  • Audio you record. Recordings are saved on your phone. They are not uploaded by Saydo. You can choose to keep audio for 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or never, or turn off keeping audio.

Information stored on your device

  • Your recordings (audio, if you keep it), transcripts, summaries, tasks, due dates, priorities, reminders, tags and the words you teach Saydo are kept in an app database and private app storage on your phone.
  • Your settings, including retention choices and the ad consent choices made through Google's consent form, are kept on your phone.
  • Saydo does not keep a copy of your data on any server.

Tasks, dates and AI-style output

The summary, tasks, dates, priorities and tags are produced on your phone by rules that read your transcript. Nothing is sent to a server to make them. These results are labelled as generated and may be wrong. If a result is wrong or offensive, you can press "Report this result". That opens an email to the developer with the transcript and task list. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your email app.

Reminders

Reminders are shown as Android notifications. Saydo asks for notification permission on Android 13 and later, when you first set a reminder. Reminders use Android's inexact alarms, so they may arrive a few minutes late. Saydo does not use exact alarms.

Export and sharing

Settings > Export JSON writes all your notes to a file on your phone and opens Android's share sheet. Sharing a note or a task list, or sending a report, hands that text to the app you choose. What that app does with it is up to that app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • What is shown. A small banner on the home screen, served by Google AdMob. Saydo can also show a rewarded video if you choose to watch one, which hides the banner for 24 hours.
  • Consent. Before any ad loads, Saydo checks with Google's User Messaging Platform whether ads may be requested. Where a consent form is required, you see it first. If the check fails, ads do not load. Where required by law, Settings shows a "Privacy options" button to change your choices.
  • What Google receives. When ads load, Google's ad SDK receives standard request data such as your IP address, device and advertising identifiers, app information and, when you consent, data used for personalised ads. Google handles this under its privacy policy.
  • Remove ads. A Saydo Pro subscription, bought through Google Play, removes the banner. Your recordings and tasks are never sent to Google as part of an ad request.

Permissions

  • Microphone. Records your voice while the recording screen is open.
  • Notifications. Shows reminders and the recording notice.
  • Internet and network state. Used only to load ads.
  • Advertising ID. Used by Google's ad SDK, as described above.
  • Boot completed. Re-arms your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service (microphone). Keeps recording running while the screen is off, with a notice.

Backups

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Saydo, so your notes and audio are not copied to your Google account backup.

Data deletion

You can delete a single note, or use Settings > Delete all to remove every note, its audio, its tasks and its tags. Auto-delete settings remove old audio and notes on a schedule. Uninstalling Saydo removes all of its data from your phone. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Saydo is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
