SamjhoDoc Privacy Policy

App: SamjhoDoc (samjhodoc) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

SamjhoDoc is a private notice organiser for bank, court, tax, school, insurance, utility, employment, property and other letters. You type the facts, optionally attach a private photo, track a deadline and read a hand-written checklist. It has no AI and no OCR: it does not read, translate or explain a letter automatically. It has no account, no developer server and no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Notice records in notices.json: title, type, sender, reference, received date, optional deadline, optional amount, your notes, status, priority and the photo file name.
  • One private JPEG copy per attached photo under files/photos, downsampled to a maximum of 1800 px and re-encoded at JPEG quality 85. The gallery original is not modified.
  • Settings in private preferences: theme, reminder switch and speech rate; the app also keeps the codes of the deadline alarms it scheduled so stale alarms can be cancelled.

The notice JSON is written with a temporary file and rename; a corrupt file is quarantined rather than silently overwritten. Draft photos are staged and unreferenced drafts are cleaned at startup. The app does not add its own encryption; the records rely on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves the device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no developer server, advertising, analytics or tracking code, so it cannot send your content to the developer or an ad network.

When you press Share, only the plain-text summary you see is handed to Android's share sheet: notice facts, deadline status, your notes and the checklist. The photo is not included. The receiving app keeps its own copy under its own policy and may use a network. SamjhoDoc itself has no upload or file-export feature.

Read aloud uses the phone's installed text-to-speech engine. The app sends the text to that system engine; the engine may be offline or may use a network voice depending on the device and its settings. That engine's own privacy policy applies. SamjhoDoc does not operate or control a TTS server.

Permissions

The app declares only POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+ runtime permission for the deadline reminders you enable) and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (normal permission used only to re-arm the next deadline alarms after a reboot, clock/time-zone change or app update). It does not request camera, storage, location, microphone, contacts or network access. The photo picker is the system picker and needs no storage permission.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Android backup and device transfer

Android automatic backup is turned off for this app (the manifest sets allowBackup=false and fullBackupContent=false). Its data-extraction rules exclude file, database, sharedpref and external domains from both cloud backup and device transfer. The developer has no server copy to restore.

No AI, no OCR and no legal authority

The checklist is static, hand-written organisational information for nine broad notice types. It is not legal, tax or financial advice and not an official requirement. The app does not know the facts of your case, the limitation period or the correct reply. Check the letter itself and consult a qualified professional or the sender.

Deadline reminders

Reminders are local and inexact: three days before a deadline and on the morning of the deadline, at the time stored by the app. Android may delay them in Doze or battery-saver mode, and a reminder is not a guarantee that a legal or financial deadline has been met.

Ads, accounts and purchases

The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no advertising, analytics or billing SDK. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

SamjhoDoc is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Deleting a notice deletes its notice record and its private photo copy. Delete all removes the library and photos. Uninstalling the app deletes its private storage. A summary you shared is outside the app and remains until you delete it in the receiving app. There is no developer server copy to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
