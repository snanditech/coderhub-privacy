TithiDay Privacy Policy

App: TithiDay (tithiday) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

TithiDay is an offline Hindu calendar and panchang. Tithi, paksha, nakshatra, sunrise, sunset, the lunar month, Samvat years and a Bengali date are calculated on the phone from the place you choose. It has no account, no developer server and no INTERNET permission. Location is chosen manually; the app does not use GPS or request a location permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps only settings in private preferences on this phone: the chosen city or custom coordinates, place label and IANA time zone; amanta/purnimanta convention; Bengali-date and fasting-day switches; daily and festival reminder switches and times; theme; and the request codes of the reminder alarms it scheduled. The panchang and festival dates are calculated on demand and are not stored as a separate record.

What leaves the device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no developer server, advertising, analytics or tracking code, so it cannot send your content to the developer or an ad network.

TithiDay has no share sheet, no export and no network feature. The selected place and reminder settings are not sent to the developer.

Permissions

The app declares only POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+ runtime permission for the reminders you enable) and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (normal permission used only to re-arm reminders after a reboot, clock/time-zone change or app update). It does not request location, storage, camera, microphone, contacts or network access. You choose a bundled city or type coordinates yourself.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Android backup and device transfer

Android automatic backup is turned off for this app (the manifest sets allowBackup=false and fullBackupContent=false). Its data-extraction rules exclude file, database, sharedpref and external domains from both cloud backup and device transfer. The developer has no server copy to restore.

Location and time zone

The app does not use GPS, network location or a location permission. It uses the city or coordinates you choose and the time zone you select. Sunrise, tithi and festival calculations depend on that choice; a wrong place or zone produces wrong-looking dates.

Accuracy and festival limits

This is a low-precision panchang, not an authority. A printed panchang can differ by minutes in a tithi/nakshatra end, and at a boundary by a day in a festival. Only 2025–2026 festival dates were checked against published panchangs; later years are rule-derived and labelled unverified. In 2026 the app's Holi rule gives 3 March while DrikPanchang keeps Rangwali Holi on 4 March because it places Holika Dahan on 3 March; the list is labelled rule-derived. Regional calendars (including Smarta/Vaishnava and West Bengal/Assam differences) can differ. Confirm important dates with your local panchang.

Reminders

Panchang and festival reminders are local and inexact. Android may delay them in Doze or battery-saver mode; they are not a guaranteed alarm.

Ads, accounts and purchases

The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no advertising, analytics or billing SDK. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

TithiDay is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

There is no saved panchang library to delete. Reset settings or clear the app's storage from Android Settings > Apps > TithiDay > Storage. Uninstalling the app deletes its settings and scheduled reminder records. There is no developer server copy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
