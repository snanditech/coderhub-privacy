Crownly Privacy Policy

App: Crownly (com.coderhub.crownly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Crownly is a single-player logic puzzle. Everything stays on your phone. Crownly has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no network connection. The developer does not run a server for Crownly, does not receive any data from it, and does not sell or share data. Crownly plays fully offline.

What Crownly stores on your device

  • Settings. Your theme (follow system, light or dark), sound effects on or off, vibration on tap on or off, and region patterns on or off.
  • Puzzle in progress. The ordinary puzzle you are working on, and the daily puzzle for the current day, so you can resume after closing the app. These are saved as text in the app's private storage.
  • Records. The number of puzzles solved, best and average times for each board size, and your daily puzzle streak. These are counts and times only. They are not linked to your name or to any identifier.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere. Crownly does not read anything else on your phone, and it does not use your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.

Daily puzzle

The daily puzzle is built on your phone from today's date, so every phone gets the same board on the same day without any download. Crownly does not send the date or your results anywhere.

Sound and vibration

Sound effects use the phone's built-in tone generator, and vibration uses the phone's haptic feedback when you tap. Both can be turned off in Settings.

Permissions

Crownly asks for no permissions. It does not request access to the internet, your location, storage, contacts, camera, microphone or notifications.

Backups

Crownly is excluded from Android's automatic app backup. Your settings, records and saved puzzles stay on the phone they were made on. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Crownly removes all of its data from your phone. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings does the same. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Crownly is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
