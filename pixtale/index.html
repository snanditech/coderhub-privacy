Pixtale Privacy Policy

App: Pixtale – Picture Logic Puzzles (com.coderhub.pixtale) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Pixtale is a nonogram (picture logic) puzzle game. Your puzzles, progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your phone. The app has no accounts, makes no network connections, contains no advertising or analytics code, and does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. The developer runs no server for Pixtale and has no copy of your data.

Information stored on your device

Pixtale saves the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Settings: your theme choice (follow system, light or dark), Sound and Vibration on or off, the difficulty filter on the menu, and whether the How to play screen has been shown.
  • Puzzles in progress: the marks on each unfinished puzzle, its elapsed time, mistakes and hints, so you can continue later.
  • Your record: the puzzles you have solved, your best time on each, whether a puzzle was solved without mistakes or without hints, and the days on which you solved the daily puzzle. This is used for the Stats screen and achievements.

None of this leaves your phone. Pixtale does not read your contacts, photos, files, location or any other app's data.

Network, ads and analytics

Pixtale does not request internet access. It does not send any information anywhere, does not show advertisements, and includes no analytics, crash-reporting or third-party SDKs. Pixtale works in aeroplane mode.

Sound and vibration

When Sound is on, Pixtale plays short tones through your phone's media volume. When Vibration is on, it uses the system haptic feedback for moves and results. Neither needs a permission, and neither records anything.

Permissions

Pixtale requests no permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage or notifications.

Backups

Android's automatic app backup is turned off for Pixtale, so your progress is not copied to Google Drive or another backup service, and it is not moved to a new phone with the app data. Your record stays on the phone where you played.

Data deletion

To delete all Pixtale data, uninstall the app, or open Android Settings > Apps > Pixtale > Storage > Clear storage. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Pixtale is rated for ages 13 and up. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
