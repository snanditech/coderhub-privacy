FieldReport Privacy Policy

App: FieldReport (com.coderhub.fieldreport) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

FieldReport is an offline app for inspections: projects, rooms, checklists, visits, issues with photos, sign-offs, and PDF and CSV reports. All of your data is stored on your device. The developer does not run any server for FieldReport, does not receive your projects, photos, signatures or reports, and does not sell or share your data. FieldReport has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

Network access

FieldReport does not request the internet permission and cannot connect to the internet. Nothing you record is uploaded.

Information stored on your device

  • Workspace. Projects, rooms, checklists, visits, checklist answers and notes, issues, due dates, status history and the local change history are saved as one file in the app's private storage.
  • Photos. Photos you take with the system camera, or pick with Android's photo picker, are copied into the app's private storage. Annotated copies are saved alongside them. The original photo is never changed by annotation.
  • Sign-offs. When you sign off a completed visit, the name you type and the signature you draw are saved in the app's private storage. The signature is stored as a PNG image.
  • Report branding. The company name and contact line you enter in Settings are saved on the device and printed on your PDF reports.
  • Settings. Theme, the optional confirmation vibration and the last report filter are kept on the device.

Reports, exports and backups

You create PDF reports, CSV files and JSON backups yourself. Each one is written only to the location you choose with Android's file picker, for example your Downloads folder or a cloud app you have installed. FieldReport does not send these files anywhere. Once a file leaves the app, what happens to it is up to you and the app or service you saved it to. A backup contains your project and its photos and signatures, and it is not encrypted by FieldReport.

Camera and photos

FieldReport does not request the camera permission. Photos are taken by the system camera app, which hands the picture back to FieldReport. Photos chosen from the gallery are read through Android's photo picker, which gives FieldReport access only to the pictures you select.

Location

FieldReport does not request location permission and does not record where photos or visits were taken.

Vibration

FieldReport uses the vibrate permission only for a short confirmation buzz when an issue or photo is saved. You can turn it off in Settings.

Deleting data

Deleting a project, visit, issue or photo in FieldReport also removes the photo and signature files that it no longer uses from the app's private storage. Files you saved outside the app, such as PDF reports, CSV files and backups, stay where you saved them; delete them there. Uninstalling FieldReport removes its private storage. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

FieldReport is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
