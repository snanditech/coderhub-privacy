Blockday Privacy Policy

App: Blockday (com.coderhub.blockday) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Blockday is a block puzzle game. It works offline. The developer does not run any server for Blockday, does not collect personal information, and does not sell or share data. Blockday has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics SDK and no network libraries. Your scores, statistics and settings stay on your phone.

Information stored on your device

  • Scores and statistics. Your best scores, today's Daily Challenge result, lifetime statistics (runs finished, lines cleared, combos, daily streak) and the achievements you have earned.
  • Game in progress. The board and pieces of the run you are playing, so you can resume it after the app is closed.
  • Settings. Theme, sound, vibration and whether the first-run guide has been shown.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent anywhere. Android's automatic app backup is turned off for Blockday, so this information is not copied to Google Drive or another cloud backup.

Daily Challenge

The Daily Challenge board is generated on your phone from the current date. Everyone who opens the game on the same date gets the same board. No information about you or your scores is sent to make this work.

Location, camera, microphone and contacts

Blockday does not ask for or use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Permissions

  • Vibrate. Used for the short buzz on a piece placement and at the end of a run. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

Blockday does not request internet access.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Blockday removes all of its data from your phone. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings does the same. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Blockday is intended for players aged 13 and older. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
