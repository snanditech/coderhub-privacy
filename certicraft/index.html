CertiCraft Privacy Policy

App: CertiCraft (certicraft) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

CertiCraft makes certificates and event, school, club or staff ID cards on Android. Templates, images and PDFs are handled on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Templates you design: titles, labels, colours, layout, QR text and the template name.
  • Images you import for a logo, signature, seal or portrait, copied into app-private storage so a revoked picker permission does not break the template.
  • CSV text you import for bulk mode, including recipient names and details.
  • Records of recent batches, and the generated PDFs and ZIPs in the app's private storage.

Templates, batch records and generated files live in the app's private storage; settings are in private preferences. Android's automatic backup is turned off for CertiCraft (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

CertiCraft does not add its own encryption to these files; they rely on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

PDFs, ZIPs and template bundles (a ZIP with the template JSON and its images) are shared through Android's share sheet from app-private files using a read-only FileProvider URI. A template bundle can also be imported through the system document picker. A copy saved by the receiving app is outside CertiCraft and remains after uninstall; that app controls it.

A CSV file is read through the system document picker with access only to the file you choose.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

QR codes and the short 'verification' code

A card can include a QR code containing the recipient, event, date and a short code computed on the device. That code is deterministic, not a secret and not a security token. Anyone who scans or reads the card sees the same text.

It does not prove that a certificate or card is genuine. CertiCraft does not verify identities, does not check attendance or qualifications, and does not create government ID documents.

Other people's details

Bulk mode imports names and details of other people. Use it only with their permission. The imported CSV, the template and the generated cards contain their data, so share and store them accordingly.

Ads, accounts and purchases

CertiCraft has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

CertiCraft is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling CertiCraft deletes its templates, imported images, CSV text, batch records and generated files from the app's private storage. You can also clear them from Android Settings > Apps > CertiCraft > Storage > Clear storage. Template bundles, PDFs and ZIPs you exported or shared are outside the app and are not deleted; remove them where you saved them or in the receiving app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
