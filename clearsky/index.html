ClearSky Privacy Policy

App: ClearSky: AQI & UV Index (com.coderhub.clearsky) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

ClearSky shows air quality (AQI) and the UV index for the places you save. It has no accounts and no analytics SDK. Your saved places, readings and settings stay on your phone. To get data, the app sends the coordinates of your saved places, rounded to about 1 km, to Open-Meteo. The home-screen banner is served by Google AdMob. The developer does not run a server for ClearSky and does not receive your data.

Location

  • Optional. ClearSky asks for approximate location only when you tap "Use approximate location" on the Places screen. It is requested once, after a short explanation.
  • What is read. The last known approximate fix from the network or passive provider. ClearSky does not start GPS and does not use location in the background.
  • What is kept. The coordinates are rounded to two decimal places and saved as a place named "My location" on your phone. You can remove it at any time.
  • Denying location does not block the app. You can search for any city instead.

Network requests

  • Open-Meteo (air quality, UV, weather, forecasts). ClearSky sends the rounded coordinates of a saved place to Open-Meteo to get the current readings, hourly and 7-day series, and sunrise and sunset. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • Open-Meteo city search. When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service.
  • Google AdMob (ads). The home-screen banner and the optional rewarded video are loaded from Google AdMob. Ads are loaded only after the consent process (Google's User Messaging Platform) says ads may be requested. AdMob may use your advertising ID and your device information, as described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing (optional subscription). If you subscribe to remove the banner, Google Play handles the payment and tells ClearSky whether the subscription is active. ClearSky does not receive your payment details.

Open-Meteo and Google handle these requests under their own terms and privacy policies. Open-Meteo terms.

Information stored on your device

  • Saved places (name and rounded coordinates).
  • A cache of readings for each place, with the time each reading was fetched. Readings older than 14 days are deleted.
  • Settings: AQI scale, theme, alert levels, the rewarded-ad window and the subscription state.
  • Alert state: whether the last reading was above each alert level, so the same alert is not sent again.

Everything stored on the device is in the app's private storage. Backup to the cloud is turned off for ClearSky.

Notifications and widget

  • Alerts. Off by default. When you turn them on, ClearSky asks for notification permission. A notification is sent when the AQI or UV index for your primary place reaches the level you chose. Checks run about every 3 hours when the phone is online.
  • Widget. The home-screen widget shows the cached AQI and UV for your primary place. It does not send any data.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. To fetch the data and to check whether you are online.
  • Approximate location. Only for the optional "Use approximate location" button.
  • Notifications. Only when alerts are turned on.
  • Advertising ID. Declared for Google AdMob. Used only for ads after consent.
  • Background work (WorkManager). The periodic refresh of the primary place, which updates the cache, the widget and alerts.

Children

ClearSky is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The banner is served by Google AdMob.

Data deletion

Settings > Data > Delete all data removes saved places, cached readings, forecasts and settings from the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. The developer holds no copy of your data.

Health information

ClearSky gives general information about air quality and sun exposure. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
