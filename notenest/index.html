NoteNest Privacy Policy

App: NoteNest – Markdown Notes (com.coderhub.notenest) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

NoteNest is a Markdown notebook. Your notes are saved as Markdown files in the app's private storage on your phone. The developer does not run a server for NoteNest, does not receive your notes or attachments, and does not sell or share your data. NoteNest has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

Internet access

NoteNest does not request the internet permission and does not connect to the internet itself. If you tap a web link inside a note, Android opens that link in your browser or another app you choose. That browser or app is then governed by its own privacy policy.

Permissions

NoteNest does not request any Android permission. Files you attach, import or export are picked with Android's system file picker, which gives the app access only to the files or folder you select.

Information stored on your device

  • Notes. Each note is a Markdown file with a short header (title, tags, dates and attachment names) and your text, kept in the app's private storage.
  • Attachments. Files you attach are copied into the app's private storage, next to the note they belong to.
  • Revisions. The app keeps up to 30 earlier versions of each note so you can restore them.
  • Search index. A local index of note titles, text and tags, used for search. It can be rebuilt from your notes at any time.
  • Settings. Your theme, editor text size and last opened notebook are stored in the app's preferences on your phone.

Import, export and sharing

  • Export. You choose a folder with the system folder picker. NoteNest writes one Markdown file per note, with attachments, into a folder named after each notebook.
  • Import. You choose a folder with the system folder picker. NoteNest reads the Markdown files in it and copies their attachments into the app.
  • Share. Sharing a note hands its title and text, as plain Markdown, to the app you pick from the Android share sheet. NoteNest sends nothing anywhere else.

Files you export or share leave the app's private storage at your choice. What happens to them after that depends on where you put them or which app receives them.

Backup

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for NoteNest, so your notes are not copied to Google Drive or any other backup service by Android. If you use a cloud folder for an export, that folder's service handles the file under its own terms.

Deleting your data

You can delete notes, notebooks and attachments inside the app, and each note's revisions go with it. Uninstalling NoteNest removes everything the app stores on your phone. Files you exported or shared are copies outside the app; delete them in the app or folder where you saved them. The developer holds no copy of your notes, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

NoteNest is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
