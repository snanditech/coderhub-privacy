Paheli Privacy Policy

App: Paheli (paheli) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

Paheli is an offline collection of original Hindi riddles with hints, levels, a daily riddle and a two-player pass-and-play quiz. It has no account, no developer server and no INTERNET permission. The two-player mode is on one phone; there are no online rooms, Wi-Fi play, chat or leaderboards.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in private storage under files/paheli:

  • progress.json (with a local .bak copy): solved riddle ids, best correct/points per level, highest unlocked level, lifetime answered/correct counts, daily streak and a map of local dates to correct/incorrect daily results.
  • level-session.json and duel-session.json: the current level attempt or two-player quiz, including question ids, options, selected answers, hints, score and player turn.
  • Private preferences: theme and an optional larger-text setting.

The records are written atomically and keep a local backup. They are not separately encrypted; they rely on Android's app sandbox and device storage encryption.

What leaves the device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no developer server, advertising, analytics or tracking code, so it cannot send your content to the developer or an ad network.

When you choose Share, a result or the daily riddle is handed to Android's share sheet as plain text. The receiving app keeps its own copy under its own policy and may use a network; Paheli itself does not upload it.

Permissions

The app declares no Android runtime permission. It does not request storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Android backup and device transfer

Android automatic backup is turned off for this app (the manifest sets allowBackup=false and fullBackupContent=false). Its current extraction rules exclude only the database and sharedpref domains from cloud backup; the app's files/ records are not named in a separate rule. The file also has no device-transfer block. The manifest switch is the primary control, and the extraction XML should be widened if backup is ever enabled.

Daily play and content limits

The daily riddle is chosen from the local date on the device; changing the device date or time zone can affect the daily selection and streak. The 36 riddles and explanations are original content for this app. A model can check structure and references but cannot certify Hindi idiom, grammar, cultural fit or every unintended alternative reading; native Hindi review is still required. Sharing opens Android's share sheet; there is no direct WhatsApp integration.

Ads, accounts and purchases

The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no advertising, analytics or billing SDK. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

Paheli is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Reset progress in Settings; this removes the local progress and saved sessions. Clearing the app's storage from Android Settings > Apps > Paheli > Storage also deletes it. Uninstalling the app deletes its private storage. Text you shared is outside the app and remains until you delete it in the receiving app. There is no developer server copy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
