JaapCounter Privacy Policy

App: JaapCounter (jaapcounter) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

JaapCounter is an offline mala and tasbih counter for mantras, names and dhikr. Counts, targets and daily totals are kept on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Your counters: name, target and current count.
  • Daily history: the count recorded against each date, broken down by counter.
  • Settings such as theme, haptics, sound, keep-awake and automatic cycle reset.

Counters and history are two JSON files in the app's private storage and settings are private preferences. Android's automatic backup is turned off for JaapCounter (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

JaapCounter does not add its own encryption to counters or history; they rely on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

The app has no share or export feature, so it creates no copies of your data outside its own storage.

Permissions

The app declares only this permission:

  • VIBRATE — a normal install-time permission used for the count pulse and the end-of-cycle vibration. It shows no prompt and gives no access to your data.

It does not request storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's own signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Ads, accounts and purchases

JaapCounter has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

JaapCounter is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling JaapCounter deletes its counters, daily history and settings. You can also reset or delete individual counters in the app, or clear everything from Android Settings > Apps > JaapCounter > Storage > Clear storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
