MileLedger Privacy Policy

App: MileLedger (mileledger) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

MileLedger is a business mileage log for trips entered by hand or recorded with the phone's location while the recording screen is open. Distances and reports are calculated on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Trips: date, vehicle, purpose, route description, distance, optional start and end odometer readings, rate, notes and whether the trip is business or personal. A GPS trip also keeps the locations used to measure it.
  • Vehicles: name, registration and current odometer reading.
  • Rates, monthly and yearly report figures, and settings such as theme and units.

Trips, vehicles and rates are stored in one JSON file in the app's private storage and settings are private preferences. Android's automatic backup is turned off for MileLedger (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

MileLedger does not add its own encryption to trips or vehicles; they rely on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

When you export a report, a CSV or PDF is written to the app's cache and shared through a read-only FileProvider URI. If you print the PDF, it is handed to the Android print service you choose, which may keep its own copy. The files can include your vehicle registration, trip purposes, routes and distances. An app you share them to keeps its own copy under its own policy.

Location

MileLedger declares ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION. Location is read only while the GPS trip screen is open, after Android's permission prompt and an in-app explanation, and stops when you leave that screen. There is no background location permission and no automatic trip detection, so the app cannot follow you while it is closed. The coordinates are used to calculate the trip distance and are kept with that trip; they are never uploaded.

Permissions

The app declares only these permissions:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — runtime permissions used only for the optional GPS trip recording screen. You can refuse them and still log every trip by hand.

It does not request storage, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's own signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Ads, accounts and purchases

MileLedger has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

MileLedger is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling MileLedger deletes its trips, vehicles, rates and settings. You can also delete individual trips and vehicles in the app, or clear everything from Android Settings > Apps > MileLedger > Storage > Clear storage. CSV and PDF files you shared or printed are outside the app and remain until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
