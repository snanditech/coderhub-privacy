Roomy Privacy Policy

App: Roomy: Storage & Photo Cleaner (com.coderhub.roomy) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Roomy helps you find large files, duplicate photos, blurry photos, screenshots and old media on your phone, so you can review them and move the ones you no longer need to Roomy's Trash. Roomy analyzes your media on your device. It does not upload your photos, videos or audio, does not have accounts, and does not run a server. The developer does not receive your files, hashes or scan results.

Media access

  • Photos, videos and audio. Roomy asks for read access to images, videos and audio (Android 13 and newer) so it can list them and compute the analysis described below. Denying one type only skips that type. You can also grant access to a subset of photos where Android offers it.
  • Screenshots. Roomy lists images in the Screenshots folder from the same media access. It does not read other files.
  • No broad file access. Roomy does not request access to all files on your device.

What Roomy computes on your device

  • Exact duplicates. A SHA-256 digest of each file up to 20 MB, so byte-identical copies can be grouped.
  • Near duplicates and blur. A small grayscale copy of each image is used to compute a perceptual hash and a focus score. The original image is not kept.
  • Sizes and dates. File size, type, dimensions and the date the file was added, as reported by Android.

These results are stored in Roomy's private database on your phone. They are used only inside the app.

Trash and deletion

Moving an item to Roomy's Trash only records it in the app's database for 30 days, so you can restore it. Your file is not moved or deleted at that point. Removing files from the Trash asks Android to delete them, and Android shows its own confirmation. On Android versions without that confirmation, Roomy asks you to confirm in the app before it deletes anything. Roomy never deletes a file without your confirmation.

Notifications

If you turn on the weekly reminder, Roomy checks the results stored on your phone once a week and posts one notification when there are files to review. This check runs on your device and sends nothing anywhere. Notifications need your permission, which you can turn off at any time in Android settings or in Roomy's Settings.

Ads and purchases

  • Banner ad. Roomy shows a banner ad on the home screen. Ads are requested through Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising identifier and information about your device, approximate location and app use, under Google's own policies. You can read them at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Consent. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent before ads are requested. You can change your choice in Roomy's Settings when the option is available.
  • Rewarded video. You can choose to watch a video to hide the banner for 24 hours.
  • Subscription. An optional monthly subscription, handled by Google Play Billing, hides the banner. Google Play processes the payment and gives Roomy only the subscription status.

Roomy does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not sell or share your data.

Information stored on your device

  • The scan database (file identifiers, names, sizes, dates, digests, perceptual hashes, focus scores and the Trash list) in Roomy's private storage.
  • Settings: theme, consent choice, subscription status, the 24-hour ad-free time and the reminder switch.

Roomy is not included in Android's automatic cloud backup or device-to-device transfer, so this data is not copied to your Google account.

Permissions

  • Images, videos and audio. To list and analyze your media, described above.
  • Notifications. For the optional weekly reminder.
  • Internet and network state. Only for ads (Google AdMob and consent) and the optional subscription. The scan and review features work offline.
  • Advertising ID. Used by Google AdMob for ads, as described above.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data clears Roomy's scan results, Trash list and settings. It does not delete your photos, videos or audio. Uninstalling Roomy also removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Roomy is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
