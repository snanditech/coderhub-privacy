Zindagi Privacy Policy

App: Zindagi (zindagi) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

Zindagi is an India-context text life simulator for Android. Events, choices and endings run entirely on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • The life in progress: age, attributes, pending event, earlier decisions and the random sequence needed to resume exactly.
  • A summary of each completed life (up to 100), the last summary and the endings gallery with dates.
  • Settings such as the theme and whether numeric attribute changes are shown.

These are JSON files and private preferences in the app's storage. Android's automatic backup is turned off for Zindagi (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

You can share a life summary as plain text through Android's share sheet. The receiving app keeps its own copy under its own policy.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

About the content

All events and endings are original fiction written for this app. A simulated life is a game, not advice about money, health or relationships, and the choices you make are not sent anywhere.

Ads, accounts and purchases

Zindagi has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

Zindagi is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Zindagi deletes the active life, completed lives, endings gallery and settings with the app's private storage. You can also clear them from Android Settings > Apps > Zindagi > Storage > Clear storage. Shared summary text is outside the app and remains with the app that received it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
