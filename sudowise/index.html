Sudowise Privacy Policy

App: Sudowise (com.coderhub.sudowise) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Sudowise is a Sudoku game. It works fully offline. Your puzzles, progress, settings and statistics are stored only on your phone. The developer does not run any server for Sudowise, does not collect your data, and does not sell or share it. Sudowise has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK, and it does not ask for any permission.

Information stored on your device

  • Game in progress. The board you are playing, including your entries, pencil marks, mistakes, hints and time, so you can continue after closing the app.
  • Statistics. Games started and solved per difficulty, your best solve time, and your daily puzzle record (days solved and streak).
  • Settings. Theme, sound, haptic feedback and the mistake-checking switch.

This information is kept in the app's private storage. It is not sent anywhere. Android's automatic backup is turned off for Sudowise, so this information is not copied to your Google account backup.

Network

Sudowise does not use the internet. The app does not request network access. Puzzles are generated on your phone, and the daily puzzle is created on your phone from today's date, so it is the same for everyone who plays that day without any server.

Sound and vibration

Sudowise can play short system sounds and haptic taps when you enter a digit or finish a puzzle. You can turn each one off in Settings. These are produced on your phone and nothing is recorded.

Permissions

Sudowise does not request any permission.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Sudowise removes all of its data from your phone. You can also clear the app's data in Android settings. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Sudowise is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
