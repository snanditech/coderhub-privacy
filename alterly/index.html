Alterly Privacy Policy

App: Alterly (com.coderhub.alterly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 24 October 2026

Summary

Alterly lets you run a second copy ("clone") of an app on your phone, so you can sign in with another account. Everything happens on your device. The developer does not run any server for Alterly, does not receive any data from it, and does not sell or share your data. Alterly has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK.

The apps you clone

  • Their data stays separate and on your phone. Each clone keeps its own files, settings and logins in Alterly's private storage. The original app and other clones cannot see them.
  • They talk to their own services. A cloned app works like the app itself: when it goes online, it connects to its own servers under its own privacy policy. Alterly does not read, change or collect that traffic.
  • Their permissions. Android grants permissions to Alterly as a whole, so a permission you allow for one clone (for example the camera) is available to the other clones too. Alterly asks for them only when you open a clone that uses them, and once per clone.

The list of your apps

To let you choose which app to clone, Alterly reads the list of apps installed on your phone (names, icons and package names). This list is used only on your device, to show the picker. It is never sent anywhere.

Information stored on your device

  • the clones and their data (see above);
  • each clone's name, badge colour and number, and its lock, hidden, guest, notification and screenshot settings;
  • your app settings (theme, language, app lock);
  • your PIN, stored only as a salted one-way hash (it cannot be turned back into the PIN). Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; Alterly never sees your biometric data;
  • a local diagnostics log for each clone, used only on the Diagnostics screen.

Guest clones lose all their data every time they close. You can reset or delete any clone in the app, and uninstalling Alterly removes everything it stored.

Notifications

Notifications from a cloned app are shown under Alterly's name. If you turn it on, Alterly adds the clone's name and colour to them, mutes them, or keeps them silent during quiet hours. This happens on your phone; notification content is not stored or sent anywhere.

Backup

You can save a clone's data as a ZIP file to a place you pick with Android's file picker (on your phone or a cloud app such as Google Drive). The file is written there by Android and the app you picked. Alterly never receives your cloud account details. The ZIP is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private. Alterly's data is excluded from Android's own cloud backup.

Information that leaves your device

Only what you choose: a backup you save, a diagnostics report you export, or something a cloned app sends to its own service. Alterly itself sends nothing to the developer.

Permissions

  • Query installed apps: listing the apps you can clone, on your device only.
  • Camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos and media, Bluetooth, nearby devices, activity and body sensors: declared so that cloned apps that need them can work. Alterly asks for them only when a clone needs them, and you can say no.
  • Internet and network state: used by the cloned apps to reach their own services.
  • Notifications and foreground service: showing the cloned apps' notifications and running their background work.
  • Biometric: optional fingerprint or face unlock for the app lock.

Data deletion

Delete or reset a clone in the app at any time, or uninstall Alterly to remove everything. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Alterly is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
