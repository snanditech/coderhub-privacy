Tripday Privacy Policy

App: Tripday – Travel Day Planner (com.coderhub.tripday) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Tripday is a trip organizer. You keep your trips, bookings, documents, checklists, reminders and expenses on your phone. Tripday has no accounts and no server that receives your trip data. The developer does not collect, receive or sell your trip information. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and offers an optional subscription through Google Play. Those two services are described below.

Information stored on your device

  • Trips and bookings. Trip names, dates, time zones, booking titles, times, addresses and confirmation codes you enter, and any text you paste or share into the app. They are kept in the app's private database.
  • Documents. Images and PDF files you add, such as tickets and vouchers. They are copied into the app's private storage. Android keeps this storage available only to Tripday. The app does not add its own encryption.
  • Checklists and expenses. Packing and task items you create, and expense names, amounts, currency codes and dates you enter.
  • Settings. Theme, home time zone, reminder setting, consent answer and subscription status, kept in the app's private storage.

Tripday does not read your photos, contacts, calendar, call log or messages, and it does not use your location.

Notifications and reminders

If you allow notifications, Tripday shows reminders before your bookings start. The reminders are scheduled on your phone with Android alarms. Tripday asks Android for permission to schedule exact alarms so reminders arrive on time. After your phone restarts, Tripday schedules the pending reminders again. Reminder times are stored on your phone only.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Tripday shows a banner on its home screen and offers a rewarded video that hides the banner for 24 hours. These ads are served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), which uses the advertising ID and other device information under Google's own privacy policy. Before any ad is requested, Tripday asks Google's User Messaging Platform for consent where the law requires it. Ads are requested only when that consent check allows it. If you are in a region that requires it, Settings shows a Privacy options entry where you can change your choice at any time.

Google's ad privacy information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and Google Privacy Policy.

Subscription (Google Play Billing)

The optional Pro subscription is bought and managed through Google Play. Google handles the payment and the account details. Tripday receives only whether a subscription is active, and it checks that status with Google Play when the app starts. Pro removes the home-screen banner.

Sharing

When you share your itinerary, Tripday creates plain text on your phone and passes it to the app you choose from Android's share sheet. What happens to the text after that is up to the app you choose. Shared documents are handled the same way when you open them from Tripday.

Network use

Tripday connects to the internet only for ads, the subscription check and the consent check described above. Trip planning, bookings, documents, reminders, expenses and the widget all work without a connection.

Backups

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Tripday, so your trips and documents are not copied to your Google account backup.

Permissions

  • Notifications. To show booking reminders.
  • Exact alarms. To send reminders at the time you set.
  • Run at startup. To schedule pending reminders again after a restart.
  • Internet and network state. For ads, the consent check and the subscription check.
  • Advertising ID. Used by Google AdMob to serve ads.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes all trips, bookings, documents, checklists, expenses, reminders and settings from the app. Uninstalling Tripday also removes its private data from your phone. Google Play keeps its own records of any subscription you bought; manage or cancel it in Google Play. The developer holds no copy of your trip data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Tripday is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
