Clipnest Privacy Policy

App: Clipnest – Smart Clipboard (com.coderhub.clipnest) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Clipnest is a clipboard history and text tool app. It stores the text you choose to save on your phone, in the app's private storage. The developer does not run any server for Clipnest, does not receive your clips, and does not sell or share your data. Clipnest has no accounts and no analytics SDK. It shows one small banner ad on the Home screen, which Google's ad SDK serves. You can remove the banner (see Advertising below).

What Clipnest saves on your device

  • Clips. Text you save with "Capture now", the "Save clipboard" Quick Settings tile, or Share to Clipnest from another app, plus text you create with transforms. Clips are kept in an app database in the app's private storage until you delete them.
  • Sensitive clips. Clipnest flags text that looks like a password, API key or card number. Flagged clips are deleted automatically 10 minutes after they are saved.
  • One-time codes. Text that looks like a one-time verification code is not saved.
  • Snippets. Snippets, their shortcuts and their {placeholder} names are kept in the app database.
  • Settings. Theme, the vault PIN (stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash, never as the PIN itself), and ad and subscription status are kept in the app's private settings storage.

Clipnest does not read your clipboard in the background. Android does not allow it, and Clipnest does not try. The clipboard is read only when you tap "Capture now" in the app, or tap the Quick Settings tile, while you are using the phone.

Sharing text into Clipnest

When you share text to Clipnest from another app, Clipnest saves that text as a clip, in the same way as the other capture actions.

Advertising

Clipnest shows a small banner on the Home screen, and offers a rewarded video that turns the banner off for 24 hours. Both are served by Google's Mobile Ads SDK. Google may use your device's advertising ID and general information such as your approximate region to choose ads. Clipnest does not send your clips or any text you have saved to the ad service.

  • Consent. Before any ad is requested, Clipnest uses Google's User Messaging Platform to collect your consent choices where the law requires it. If your choices do not allow ads, no ad is loaded.
  • Privacy options. Where required, Settings shows a "Privacy options" button that lets you change your consent choices at any time.
  • Remove ads. Watching a rewarded video turns the banner off for 24 hours. Clipnest Pro, a monthly subscription, turns it off while the subscription is active.

Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Subscription

Clipnest Pro is a monthly subscription bought through Google Play. Google Play handles payment and processes the purchase under its own terms. Clipnest receives only whether a subscription is active, so it can turn off the banner. It does not receive your payment details or your name.

Network use

Clipnest needs the internet only for the ad banner, the rewarded video, UMP consent checks and the optional subscription. Saving, searching, transforms, snippets and the vault work without a connection. Clipnest does not upload your clips.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Ads, consent and subscription checks described above.
  • Advertising ID. Used by the ad SDK, as described above.

Clipnest does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, notifications or accessibility access.

Backup

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Clipnest, so your clips are not copied to your Google account backup.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes all clips, snippets, templates and settings from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us. Google handles any advertising data under its own policy; you can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children

Clipnest is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
