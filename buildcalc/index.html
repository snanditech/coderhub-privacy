BuildCalc Privacy Policy

App: BuildCalc (buildcalc) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

BuildCalc turns room sizes into material quantities and cost estimates for building work. All calculations run on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Saved projects: rooms and their dimensions, chosen materials, quantities, the rates used at the time, currency and the calculated totals.
  • Your edited rate card and settings such as theme and metric or imperial units.

Projects are one JSON file in the app's private storage and settings are private preferences. Android's automatic backup is turned off for BuildCalc (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

BuildCalc does not add its own encryption to projects or rates; they rely on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

When you export an estimate, an itemised A4 PDF is written to the app's cache and shared as a read-only FileProvider URI. If you print, the same PDF is handed to the Android print service you choose, which may keep its own copy. The PDF contains the project name, rooms, quantities, rates and totals. An app you share it to keeps its own copy under its own policy.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android runtime permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's own signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Ads, accounts and purchases

BuildCalc has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

BuildCalc is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling BuildCalc deletes its projects, rate card and settings. You can also delete individual projects in the app, or clear everything from Android Settings > Apps > BuildCalc > Storage > Clear storage. PDFs you shared or printed are outside the app and remain until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
