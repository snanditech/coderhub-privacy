Ownly Privacy Policy

App: Ownly – Home Inventory (com.coderhub.ownly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Ownly is a home inventory and warranty app. You record your items, their rooms, categories, prices, purchase dates, warranty end dates, photos, receipts and maintenance tasks. Everything you enter is stored on your phone. The developer does not run a server for Ownly, does not receive your items, photos or receipts, and does not sell or share your data. Ownly has no accounts and no analytics SDK. It does show ads, described below.

Information stored on your device

  • Inventory records. Item names, rooms, categories, stores, prices, purchase and warranty dates, barcodes, notes and maintenance tasks are kept in Ownly's private database on your phone.
  • Photos and receipts. Photos and receipt images you add are copied into Ownly's private storage on your phone. Ownly does not use your gallery or the original picked file afterwards.
  • Settings. Theme, reminder setting, ad consent answers from Google's consent form, and subscription status are kept in the app's private settings storage.

On-device processing

Barcode reading and text recognition on receipts use Google ML Kit. These run on your phone. Images are not sent to Google or anyone else for this purpose, and no API key is used. Text read from a receipt is saved with the receipt only if you save the item.

Warranty reminders

Ownly checks once a day with Android's WorkManager whether any warranty is expiring soon or any maintenance task is due. If so, it shows a notification on your phone. You can turn reminders off in Settings. Ownly asks for notification permission for this purpose only.

Backups you create

You can create a backup file and restore from one. Ownly writes the backup to a location you choose with Android's file picker, for example on your phone or in a cloud app such as Google Drive. The file is encrypted on your phone with AES-256 using a password you choose, with the key derived by PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256. The developer does not receive the backup or the password, and cannot recover either. Restoring a backup replaces the data on your phone. Ownly does not upload backups itself, and Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Ownly.

Reports you export

You can save a PDF or CSV inventory report to a location you choose. The report is created on your phone. It contains your item records and, in the PDF, the first photo of each item. What happens to a report after you save or share it is up to you and the app or service you use.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ownly shows a small banner ad on the home screen, and offers a rewarded video that turns the banner off for 24 hours. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Ownly requests ads only when Google's User Messaging Platform says ads may be requested for you. Where the consent form applies, you can change your choices from Settings > Privacy options. AdMob may use the advertising ID of your device, and handles requests under its own policies: Google privacy policy. If consent is not given where required, no ads are requested.

Subscription

An optional monthly subscription removes the home screen banner. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. Ownly receives only whether the subscription is active. It does not receive your payment details.

Internet access

Ownly uses the internet only for ads (Google AdMob) and for checking the subscription status with Google Play. Your inventory, photos, receipts, reports and backups do not need a network connection.

Permissions

  • Notifications. To show warranty and maintenance reminders.
  • Internet and network state. For ads and subscription checks described above.
  • Advertising ID. Used by Google AdMob to serve ads, subject to your consent choices.

Ownly does not ask for camera, microphone, location or contacts access. Photos and receipts are picked with Android's photo picker, which gives Ownly access only to the images you choose.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes every item, room, photo, receipt, maintenance task and setting stored by Ownly on your phone. Backup files you created are stored where you saved them, and you can delete them there. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Ownly is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
