Whospy Privacy Policy

App: Whospy (whospy) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

Whospy is a pass-and-play spy party game for three to ten people around one phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Settings only: the theme and the discussion timer length.

The two settings are private preferences in the app's storage. Roles, the secret place, player names and the session score are held in memory for the round and are not written to a file. Android's automatic backup is turned off for Whospy (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy these settings to Google Drive.

Whospy stores no names, roles or places, so there is no record to encrypt beyond the two settings. Android's app sandbox and device encryption protect those.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

The app has no share or export feature, so it creates no copies of your data outside its own storage.

There are no online rooms, no room codes and no way to send a role, a place or a score to another phone. Everything is spoken aloud around the one device.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android runtime permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's own signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Ads, accounts and purchases

Whospy has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

Whospy is not directed at children under 13. It is a party game for groups; the place packs use common nouns and contain no branded locations or personal data. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Whospy deletes its two settings; the round itself exists only in memory and ends when you close the app. You can also clear app storage from Android Settings > Apps > Whospy > Storage > Clear storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
