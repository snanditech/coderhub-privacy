Beacon Privacy Policy

App: Beacon: Flashlight & SOS (com.coderhub.beacon) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Beacon turns your phone's torch into a flashlight, an SOS and signal light, and a screen light. Beacon has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. It does not collect, record or share personal information. Your custom signal patterns and settings are stored only on your phone. Beacon shows a small banner from Google AdMob on two screens, and Google may use advertising identifiers for that banner, as described below.

What the app does not do

  • It does not use the camera to take photos or video. It only switches the torch LED on and off.
  • It does not use the microphone and does not record audio.
  • It does not use your location.
  • It does not read your contacts, files, photos or messages.
  • It does not send the signals you create to anyone.

Information stored on your device

  • Custom patterns. The names and Morse text of the patterns you save are kept in the app's private database on your phone.
  • Settings. Your choices, such as the auto-off timer, torch brightness, strobe settings, screen light colour and theme, are kept in the app's private storage on your phone.

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Beacon, so these items are not copied to your Google account backup. Uninstalling the app removes them. Delete all data in Settings removes them at once.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Beacon shows one banner on the Patterns and Settings screens. The torch, strobe, SOS and screen light screens show no ads. The banner is served by Google AdMob. Beacon does not request ads until the consent system (Google User Messaging Platform) says ads may be requested. Where the law requires it, a consent form is shown. You can change your choices at any time from Settings > Privacy options, if that entry is shown for you.

Google may use the advertising ID of your device and your IP address to show and measure ads, under Google's privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Beacon does not receive any data from the ad network other than the ad itself.

You can remove the banner in two ways. Pro removes it for good. Watching a short rewarded video removes it for 24 hours. Neither option gives Beacon any personal data.

Purchases

Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment and purchase records under its own terms. Beacon receives only whether a Pro subscription is active. It never receives your payment details.

Network use

Beacon uses the internet only for the ad banner, the optional rewarded video and Play Billing. The torch, signal patterns, strobe and screen light work without a connection.

Permissions

  • Foreground service. Keeps the torch and signal patterns running while the screen is off. Beacon shows a silent notification with an Off button while a session runs.
  • Notifications. Shows that notification on Android 13 and later.
  • Internet and network state. Ad banner, rewarded video and Play Billing, as described above.
  • Advertising ID. Used by the ad banner.

Beacon does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or accessibility permissions. Turning the torch on does not need a camera permission.

Children

Beacon is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The ad banner is served by Google AdMob, which has its own rules for child-directed content.

Emergencies

Beacon's SOS and other patterns are visual signals only. Beacon never contacts emergency services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number yourself.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes your custom patterns and settings from your phone. Uninstalling Beacon removes everything the app stored. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
