Chaupal Privacy Policy

App: Chaupal (com.coderhub.chaupal) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Chaupal is an offline board game for one or two players: the tigers and goats game Bagh-Chal. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no internet access, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics. The app does not send anything off your phone. Your game, your settings and your win record are stored only on your phone.

Information stored on your device

  • The game in progress. After every move, the position is saved in the app's private storage, so you can resume the game after closing the app. It is cleared when the game ends and replaced when you start a new one.
  • Settings. Your choices for side, opponent, computer difficulty, theme, sound and vibration.
  • Win record. A count of games won by goats and by tigers. This is a number only; no names or other details are kept.

This information is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. It is not uploaded and is not shared with anyone.

Permissions

  • Vibration. Used for short buzzes on moves and results. You can turn it off in Settings.

The app does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files, and it does not request internet access.

Sound

Sound effects are generated on your phone by the system's tone generator. No audio is recorded.

Backup

Android's automatic backup is turned off for Chaupal, so the game and record are not copied to cloud backup.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone. Finishing a game clears the saved game, and starting a new game replaces it. The app has no option to clear the win record on its own, so uninstalling is the way to remove it. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Chaupal is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
