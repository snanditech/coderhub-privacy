Mendi Privacy Policy

App: Mendi (mendi) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

Mendi is an offline card-game app for Mendikot and Dehla Pakad, played with a computer partner against two computer rivals. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Saved matches: hands, trump mode, tricks, heap state, scores and whose turn it is.
  • Statistics and the local Bronze to Diamond rank ladder.
  • Settings such as theme, bot difficulty, trump mode, match length and large cards.

Saved matches and statistics are JSON files in the app's private storage and settings are private preferences. Android's automatic backup is turned off for Mendi (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

Mendi does not add its own encryption to these records; they rely on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

The app has no share or export feature, so it creates no copies of your data outside its own storage.

The rank ladder is stored only on this phone. There is no online account, no online leaderboard and no match history shared with anyone.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android runtime permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's own signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Ads, accounts and purchases

Mendi has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

Mendi is not directed at children under 13. It is a game with no chat, no user-generated content feed and no way to reach other players, whether online or offline. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Mendi deletes its saves, statistics, ladder and settings. You can also clear statistics and the ladder in the app, or clear everything from Android Settings > Apps > Mendi > Storage > Clear storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
