Pourly Privacy Policy

App: Pourly: Water Sort Puzzle (com.coderhub.pourly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Pourly is an offline water sort puzzle. It does not have accounts, does not connect to the internet, does not show ads, does not include analytics or any third-party SDK, and does not collect personal information. Your settings, your saved game and your stats are kept only on your phone. The developer runs no server for Pourly and receives no data from it.

What the app stores on your device

  • Settings. Theme, sound, haptic feedback and colour patterns.
  • Game progress. The level you are on, the game in progress so it can resume after the app is closed, the best move count for each level, and how far you have cleared the levels.
  • Stats. Counts of solves and clean solves, and the days on which you solved the daily puzzle, used for the streak and the achievements.

All of this is stored in the app's private storage. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Internet, ads and analytics

Pourly does not request internet access. It sends nothing from your phone, and it shows no advertising.

Sound and vibration

Pourly plays short system tones and gives short haptic taps on pours, rejected pours and a solve. You can switch each one off in Settings. Pourly does not use the microphone or the camera.

Permissions

Pourly asks for no permissions.

Backup

Android's automatic backup is turned off for Pourly, so your saved game and stats are not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone. Reinstalling the app starts with a fresh save.

Children

Pourly is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
