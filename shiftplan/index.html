ShiftPlan Privacy Policy

App: ShiftPlan: Shift Calendar (com.coderhub.shiftplan) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 6 November 2026

Summary

ShiftPlan is a shift calendar. You describe your shift patterns, and the app shows your roster, planned and worked hours, and reminders. Your roster is stored on your phone in a private file. The developer does not run any server for ShiftPlan, does not receive your roster, and does not collect, sell or share your data. ShiftPlan has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Network access

ShiftPlan does not request the internet permission and does not make any network requests. Nothing you enter leaves your phone unless you choose to share, export or back up a file yourself, as described below.

Information stored on your device

  • Roster. Your shift templates, patterns, changes to individual days, leave, worked hours and settings are saved as one JSON file in the app's private storage.
  • Reminder schedule. Upcoming reminder times are kept by Android's alarm system, and a short list of their identifiers is kept in the app's private preferences.
  • Export files. When you share a CSV or ICS export, a copy of that file is written to the app's cache folder so Android can pass it to the app you choose. The cache folder is cleared by Android when it needs space.
  • Calendar links. When you add shifts to a calendar, ShiftPlan keeps the ids of the events it added, and the name of the calendar, in the app's private preferences. This lets it update or remove only its own events. The events themselves are stored in the calendar you chose on your phone.
  • Pay settings. If you enter an hourly rate, night and weekend percentages or overtime settings, they are saved in your roster file on your phone with the rest of your settings.

Notifications

ShiftPlan asks for permission to show notifications the first time you turn on shift reminders. A reminder is a local notification shown on your phone before each rostered shift. It contains the shift name, start and end times and the day. You can turn reminders off in ShiftPlan's Settings, or block notifications in Android's settings at any time.

Starting after a restart

ShiftPlan listens for the phone restarting, a change of time or time zone, and an app update. When one of these happens, it rebuilds your reminder schedule from the roster stored on your phone. It does not run in the background otherwise.

Home-screen widget

The optional home-screen widget shows today's shift and your next few shifts, read from the roster stored on your phone. It does not send anything anywhere. It redraws after you change your roster, after a restart or time change, and once a day just after midnight while a widget is on your home screen. Tapping the widget opens ShiftPlan.

Sharing, exporting and backup

  • Export. You can export a date range as a CSV spreadsheet or an ICS calendar file. The CSV includes an estimated pay column if you have set an hourly rate. You choose where the file goes: you can share it with an app of your choice or save it with Android's system file picker.
  • Backup and restore. You can save the whole roster as one JSON file, and restore it later from a file you pick. The file is not encrypted. Keep it somewhere you trust.
  • Once a file leaves ShiftPlan, the app or service you sent it to handles it under its own terms.

Calendar

On the Export tab you can add the shifts in a date range to a calendar on your phone, update them later, change the calendar, or remove them. ShiftPlan asks for calendar access the first time you tap one of these buttons, after a short explanation. It reads the list of calendars so you can choose one, and it adds, updates or removes only the shift events it added. ShiftPlan does not send calendar data anywhere. If the calendar you choose is synced to an online account, such as Google Calendar, that account's own sync copies the events there under its own terms.

Android backup

Android's own cloud backup is turned off for ShiftPlan, so your roster is not copied to your Google account backup. Use Backup in the app to keep a copy.

Permissions

  • Notifications. Shows the shift reminders you turn on.
  • Exact alarms. Lets reminders arrive at the time you chose. Android may ask you to allow this in system settings.
  • Run at startup. Rebuilds the reminder schedule and the widget after a restart, a time change or an app update.
  • Calendar (read and write). Asked only when you tap a calendar button on the Export tab. Lets ShiftPlan list your calendars and add, update or remove its own shift events.

ShiftPlan does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, files outside the picker you choose, camera or microphone.

Data deletion

To delete your data, clear ShiftPlan's storage in Android's app settings or uninstall the app. Shift events added to a calendar stay in that calendar until you use Remove ShiftPlan events in the app, or delete them in your calendar app. Files you exported or backed up are yours and stay where you saved them; delete them in the app or folder where they are kept. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

ShiftPlan is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
