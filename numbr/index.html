Numbr Privacy Policy

App: Numbr – Scientific Calculator (com.coderhub.numbr) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Numbr is a scientific calculator. Calculations, history, memory, settings and the graph all run on your phone and work without a network connection. The developer does not run a server for Numbr, does not receive your expressions, results or history, and does not sell or share your data. Numbr has no accounts and no analytics SDK. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and the consent form described below controls them.

What stays on your device

  • History. Each completed calculation (expression, result, time and any label you add) is saved in a private database in the app's storage.
  • Memory. The value stored with MS, M+ and M−.
  • Settings. Angle mode, precision, separators, theme, haptics, result format and your ad-free status, kept in the app's private preferences.
  • Clipboard. When you tap Copy result, the shown value is placed on the system clipboard. Numbr does not read the clipboard.

Android's cloud backup is turned off for Numbr, so this data is not uploaded to Google Drive. You can delete all of it in Settings > Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Numbr shows a small banner on the calculator screen. The banner and an optional rewarded video are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

  • Consent. Before any ad is requested, Numbr asks Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) whether ads may be requested in your region. If UMP reports that they may not be requested, no ad is loaded. Numbr shows the consent form when UMP requires it, and you can change your choice later through the privacy options that Google provides.
  • Data sent to Google. When ads are allowed, the ad SDK sends Google the information it needs to serve an ad, such as your IP address, device and app details, and an advertising identifier that you can reset or remove in Android settings. Google's use of this data is described in Google's privacy policy for its partners. Numbr does not receive or store any advertising identifier.
  • Ad-free options. You can watch an optional rewarded video to turn off the banner for 24 hours, or subscribe to remove it (see below).

Subscription (Google Play Billing)

The optional Numbr subscription is bought through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment and tells Numbr whether a subscription is active. Numbr stores only that active or inactive status on your phone and checks it with Google Play when it starts. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or any other purchase information. Manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play app.

Permissions

  • Internet. Used only to load ads and to check the subscription with Google Play. Calculations never use the network.
  • Network state. Used by the ad SDK to check whether a connection exists.
  • Advertising ID. Declared for the ad SDK. Numbr does not read it.

Numbr does not ask for location, camera, contacts, storage, microphone or notification permission.

Children

Numbr is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads shown to children are controlled by the consent choices made in the UMP form and by Google's own policies.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes the history, memory and settings stored by Numbr. Uninstalling the app removes the same data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us. Google's ad and purchase records are held by Google under its own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
