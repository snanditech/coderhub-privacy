Jobpilot Privacy Policy

App: Jobpilot – Job Application Tracker (com.coderhub.jobpilot) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Jobpilot is a job application tracker. You add applications, move them through a pipeline, keep interview notes, set follow-up reminders and keep resume text. Everything is stored on your phone. The developer does not run any server for Jobpilot, does not receive your applications, notes, resumes or contacts, and does not sell or share your data. Jobpilot has no accounts and no analytics SDK. It shows ads (see below).

Information stored on your device

  • Applications. Company, role, status, link, location, salary range, contact name, notes and dates.
  • Interview rounds. Round name, questions, your answers, and an optional interview date and time.
  • Reminders. The reminder text and the date it is due.
  • Resumes. A resume PDF you choose is read on your phone to extract its text. The extracted text and a label are kept in the app's database. The PDF itself is not copied into the app. Jobpilot does not upload resumes.
  • Profile and settings. Your name, email and target role if you enter them, plus theme and notification settings.

This data stays in the app's private storage. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Jobpilot, so the data is not copied to your Google account backup.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Jobpilot shows follow-up reminders you set. Reminders use Android's alarm service, which can be delayed by a few minutes to save battery. You can turn reminders off in Settings, or in Android's notification settings for the app. Jobpilot does not use exact alarms.

Import and export of your applications

You can export all applications to a CSV file, and import a CSV file, using Android's file picker. The file goes to the place you choose. Jobpilot does not upload it. Anything you do with the file afterwards is up to you and the app you open it with.

Sharing a job link into Jobpilot

When you share text from another app to Jobpilot, Jobpilot reads the link and any title in that text and uses them to start a new application. Jobpilot does not open or download the link.

Ads

Jobpilot shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a small banner on the home screen and an optional rewarded video that hides the banner for 24 hours. Before ads are requested, Jobpilot uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it. If you are in a region where consent can be changed, a "Privacy options" entry in Settings lets you change it. Ads are only requested when the consent platform allows it. The ad SDK sends requests to Google over the internet, including your IP address, device identifiers and ad interaction data, under Google's privacy policy. Jobpilot does not send your applications, notes or resumes to Google.

Subscription

An optional monthly subscription through Google Play removes the banner. Google Play handles payment and sends Jobpilot only whether a subscription is active. Jobpilot does not receive your payment details.

Internet use

Jobpilot does not need an account and does not upload your data. The only internet use is for ads and for checking the Play subscription described above.

Permissions

  • Notifications. To show follow-up reminders. Asked when you first set a reminder or allow notifications in Settings.
  • Receive boot completed. To set reminders again after your phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state. For ads and the subscription check.
  • Advertising ID. Used by the ad SDK.

Jobpilot does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes your applications, notes, reminders, resumes, profile and settings from the app, and cancels pending reminders. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. Files you exported are yours and stay where you saved them. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Jobpilot is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
