BunkMeter Privacy Policy

App: BunkMeter (bunkmeter) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

BunkMeter is an offline college attendance tracker. It stores the subjects, timetable and attendance records you enter on this device. It has no account, no developer server and no INTERNET permission, so it cannot upload your records. The optional daily reminder is a local Android notification.

What the app stores on your device

BunkMeter keeps its attendance database in its private storage under files/bunkmeter:

  • attendance.json: your subjects (name, attendance target and archived flag), timetable slots (weekday, start and end time), and attendance records (subject, local date, timetable slot, present/absent/cancelled status, and an optional note of up to 500 characters).
  • attendance.previous.json: a previous complete copy of the same database, kept so the app can restore it if the main file is damaged.
  • attendance.json.tmp: a short-lived temporary file used while saving.
  • attendance.erase: a marker file written while an erase is being completed. If the app is interrupted, the next launch finishes the erase and does not load the old copies.

Private app preferences store the theme, the default target for new subjects, whether the daily reminder is enabled, and the reminder time.

Subject names and notes are free text. You control what you type, and you should avoid entering information you do not want stored on the device. BunkMeter does not request location, contacts, calendar, camera, microphone or storage access, and it does not read your college portal or any other app.

The records are not separately encrypted by BunkMeter. They rely on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves the device

Nothing is uploaded or shared. BunkMeter declares no INTERNET permission, has no developer server, and contains no advertising, analytics, account or billing code. There is no export or share feature. The optional reminder is a local notification generated by Android on the device, and tapping it opens BunkMeter.

Permissions

BunkMeter declares two Android permissions:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — requested only when you turn on the daily reminder on Android 13 or later. If you deny it, the reminder stays off and BunkMeter explains how to allow notifications in Android settings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — a normal permission used to restore an enabled reminder after the device restarts. The same receiver also handles app-update, clock-change and timezone-change broadcasts.

BunkMeter does not request an exact-alarm permission, location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permission. Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION; it is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Daily reminder and wake-up limits

The reminder is one local notification per day at the time you choose. BunkMeter uses a one-shot inexact Android alarm and schedules the next occurrence after each delivery, so the chosen local time is kept across normal day-to-day use and daylight-saving changes. It does not use an exact alarm.

Delivery is not guaranteed. Android may delay an inexact alarm because of Doze, battery restrictions, device policy or manufacturer power management, and the notification will not wake the device at an exact second. If notifications are denied, blocked or turned off, no reminder is posted. Restarting the device or changing the clock or timezone re-arms an enabled reminder, but it still depends on Android allowing the broadcast and the notification. The notification text is generic and does not include subject names, attendance percentages or notes.

Android backup and device transfer

Android automatic backup is turned off for BunkMeter: the manifest sets allowBackup=false and fullBackupContent=false. The app's extraction rules also exclude the database and sharedpref domains from cloud backup, but they do not include a separate device-transfer block and do not name the files/ records individually. The manifest switch is the primary control.

This policy does not claim that a manufacturer's device-to-device transfer tool, a rooted device or a forensic copy cannot access app data. To remove the app's data reliably, clear its storage in Android settings or uninstall it. If backup behaviour is ever re-enabled, the extraction rules should be widened before this page can claim that app files are excluded.

The attendance.previous.json copy is a local recovery copy, not a Google or developer backup. BunkMeter restores it if the main database is unreadable; if both copies are unreadable, the app offers a recovery screen instead of silently overwriting them.

Deletion

You can delete individual subjects, timetable slots and attendance records in the app, and some record deletions can be undone during the current session.

Settings offers two explicitly confirmed actions:

  • Delete attendance records removes every subject, timetable slot and attendance record, plus attendance.previous.json and every app-owned temporary copy. Your reminder, theme and default target stay.
  • Erase attendance and reminder does the same file erase, turns off the daily reminder, cancels its pending alarm and resets the reminder time to 08:00. Theme and default-target settings stay.

An erase writes attendance.erase before deleting the data copies and removes that marker only after cleanup succeeds. If the process is interrupted, the next launch finishes the erase and does not load or restore the old records. If a copy cannot be removed after the marker is written, BunkMeter reports that the erase did not complete instead of claiming success, and the app will not read the old copies; retry, or clear the app's storage in Android settings. If the marker itself cannot be written, the old data stays intact and the action also reports failure. A failed erase can therefore leave a copy on the device.

To remove all BunkMeter data, including theme and default-target settings, clear the app's storage from Android Settings > Apps > BunkMeter > Storage, or uninstall the app. There is no developer server copy to delete. BunkMeter can remove its own files and recovery copies so the app will not restore them, but it cannot guarantee that flash storage or copies outside its private app storage have been physically overwritten.

Ads, accounts and purchases

BunkMeter has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no advertising, analytics or billing SDK. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

BunkMeter is intended for college students and is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
