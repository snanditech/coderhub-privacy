Sweeply Privacy Policy

App: Sweeply (com.coderhub.sweeply) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 17 November 2026

Summary

Sweeply is a minesweeper game. It has no accounts, no in-app purchases and no analytics of its own. The game is fully playable offline, and it works in aeroplane mode. The menu and stats screens show a small banner ad from Google AdMob. The ads are not shown during play. The developer does not run a server for Sweeply and receives no data from it. Your game progress stays on your phone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Sweeply shows banner ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google. Banners appear only on the main menu and the stats screen, never on the board while you play.

Google's ad SDK may collect and use the following to show ads, prevent fraud and measure ads:

  • your advertising ID (a resettable identifier you can reset or delete in your Android settings);
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • device information and app-interaction data.

Google handles this data under its own policies. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Sweeply itself does not read your location.

Consent

Where required by law, such as in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Sweeply uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads are requested. If you did not consent, ads are not requested. Where UMP reports that you can change your ad choices, a "Ad choices" button appears in Settings, and you can change them there at any time.

Information stored on your device

  • Settings. Theme, sound, vibration, the solvable-boards option, and the size of your last custom board.
  • The game in progress. The board, your flags and open cells, and the elapsed time. It is saved after each move so you can continue after Android closes the app. It is removed when the game ends.
  • Best times and statistics. Your best time for each preset board, and for each board size the number of finished games, wins and win streaks.

This information is kept in the app's private storage. Your game progress, statistics and best times are not sent anywhere. Sweeply does not read contacts, photos, location or any other data from your phone.

Sound and vibration

Sound uses the phone's built-in tone generator. Vibration uses the standard haptic feedback of the system, so it needs no special permission. Both can be switched off in Settings.

Permissions

Sweeply asks for internet access and network-state access, which are used only to load the banner ad. It also declares the advertising ID permission used by Google's ad SDK. It asks for no other permissions, and the game itself makes no network requests.

Backup

Android's automatic backup is turned off for Sweeply, so your saved game and statistics are not copied to cloud backups.

Data deletion

Statistics > Reset statistics and best times clears your win counts, streaks and best times. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored on your phone. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Sweeply is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
