Sweeply Privacy Policy

App: Sweeply (com.coderhub.sweeply) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Sweeply is a minesweeper game. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics. It does not connect to the internet, and it works in aeroplane mode. The developer does not run a server for Sweeply and receives no data from it. Everything the app stores stays on your phone.

Information stored on your device

  • Settings. Theme, sound, vibration, the solvable-boards option, and the size of your last custom board.
  • The game in progress. The board, your flags and open cells, and the elapsed time. It is saved after each move so you can continue after Android closes the app. It is removed when the game ends.
  • Best times and statistics. Your best time for each preset board, and for each board size the number of finished games, wins and win streaks.

This information is kept in the app's private storage. It is not sent anywhere. Sweeply does not read contacts, photos, location or any other data from your phone.

Sound and vibration

Sound uses the phone's built-in tone generator. Vibration uses the standard haptic feedback of the system, so it needs no special permission. Both can be switched off in Settings.

Permissions

Sweeply asks for no permissions.

Backup

Android's automatic backup is turned off for Sweeply, so your saved game and statistics are not copied to cloud backups.

Data deletion

Statistics > Reset statistics and best times clears your win counts, streaks and best times. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored on your phone. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Sweeply is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
