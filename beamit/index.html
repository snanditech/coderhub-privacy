Beamit Privacy Policy

App: Beamit – Local File Transfer (com.coderhub.beamit) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Beamit moves files between your phone and another device on the same Wi-Fi network. It runs a small web server on your phone while you are sharing. The other device opens a link or scans a QR code in its browser, and the files travel directly between the two devices on your local network. The developer does not run any server for file transfers, does not receive your files, file names or transfer history, and does not sell your data. Beamit has no accounts and no sign-in.

Files and transfers stay on your local network

  • Local only. The server starts only when you tap Start sharing, only on a Wi-Fi or Ethernet network, and only on the phone's local network address. It answers only devices on the same subnet. It does not start on mobile data or a VPN.
  • Stopped by you. The server runs while sharing is on, with a notification that has a Stop action. It is not restarted by Android after the app is closed.
  • No relay. Beamit never sends your files or file names to the developer or any other server.
  • Pairing code. Each session has a 6-character pairing code, shown in the app and in the QR link. Uploads to your phone always need the code. You can also require the code for downloads. Wrong codes are rate-limited.
  • Files you share are copies you choose in Android's file picker, kept in the app's private storage until you remove them.
  • Files received from another device are kept in the app's private storage until you save them to a location you choose or delete them. Partial files from a cancelled or dropped upload are deleted.
  • Transfer history. Beamit keeps a list of finished transfers on your phone: file name, size, direction, start and end time, and status. It is stored only in the app's private database. You can clear it in the app.

Network traffic from the app itself

Apart from serving files on your local network, Beamit connects to the internet only for:

  • Advertising (Google AdMob). A banner on the home screen and an optional rewarded video. Ads load only after the consent check (Google User Messaging Platform) says ads may be requested. Google may collect device and ad identifiers (the Android advertising ID) and the ad request details, under Google's privacy policy. The app is not directed at children.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form may appear. You can change your choices any time from Settings > Privacy options.
  • Subscription (Google Play Billing). Purchases and subscription status are handled by Google Play. Beamit receives only whether a subscription is active. It does not receive your payment details.

Beamit does not send file names, file contents, transfer history, device names or your local network addresses to the developer. Beamit has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Information stored on your device

  • Settings: port, pairing code, device name, theme, keep-screen-awake, and ad and subscription status.
  • Transfer history (described above).
  • Files you shared and files you received, in the app's private storage.

Beamit's Android backup is turned off, so these files are not copied to Google Drive or another cloud backup.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. To run the local server, to read the Wi-Fi state, and for the ads and subscription described above.
  • Notifications. To show the sharing notification with its Stop action.
  • Foreground service (data sync). To keep the server running while the screen is off during a transfer.
  • Advertising ID. Used by Google AdMob for ads.

Beamit does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions. Files are picked and saved with Android's file picker, which gives the app access only to the files you choose.

Sharing from other apps

When you use Beamit as a share target, the files you share are copied into Beamit's share list, as described above, and nothing is sent until you start sharing.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes the transfer history, the shared and received files, and the settings. Files you already saved to another location are yours and stay there. Uninstalling Beamit also removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data.

Children

Beamit is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
