Ludoly Privacy Policy

App: Ludoly (com.coderhub.ludoly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 10 October 2026

Summary

Ludoly does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not request internet access.

Information stored on your device

To work, Ludoly keeps a few items in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your settings (theme, sound, vibration, number of players);
  • the player names you type in and whether each seat is a person or the computer;
  • the game in progress, so you can resume it;
  • the number of wins for each colour.

This information never leaves your device through Ludoly. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Device backup

If you have turned on Android's backup service, Android may include Ludoly's settings and win record in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account and its own privacy terms; the developer cannot access it.

Permissions

Ludoly requests only the vibration permission, used for optional vibration during play.

Children

Ludoly does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
