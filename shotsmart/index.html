ShotSmart Privacy Policy

App: ShotSmart – Screenshot to Action (com.coderhub.shotsmart) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

ShotSmart reads the text in screenshots and images you choose, finds things like dates, addresses, phone numbers, links, emails, prices and tracking numbers, and lets you act on them. Text recognition runs on your phone. ShotSmart has no accounts and the developer does not run a server that receives your screenshots, text or history. The developer does not sell your data.

Two things can send data off your phone: the advertising SDK (Google AdMob) that shows the home-screen banner or a rewarded video, and Google Play when you buy a subscription. Both are described below.

Images you choose

  • How you pick them. You choose images with Android's photo picker, or share an image to ShotSmart from another app. ShotSmart does not ask for access to your whole photo library or your storage, and it does not scan your gallery in the background.
  • Batch. The batch action lets you pick up to five images at once through the same photo picker. They are processed one after another on your phone.
  • Sample images. The onboarding screen offers built-in sample images to try the app.

Text recognition and processing on your device

Text is read with Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs on your device. ShotSmart then finds dates, addresses, phone numbers, links, emails, prices and tracking numbers with rules on your device. Neither the image nor the text is sent to the developer or to any AI or cloud service. ShotSmart does not use a cloud AI model.

Information stored on your device

  • Analyses. For each analysis ShotSmart keeps a copy of the image, a smaller thumbnail, the extracted text, the detected items, the title, the time, and any note you add. These are kept in ShotSmart's private app storage and in a private database.
  • Actions. A short record of the actions you tap (for example "Add to calendar" or "Copy") is kept with the analysis so you can see it in its history.
  • Settings. Onboarding status, subscription status and the rewarded-video window are kept in app preferences.
  • Backups. Android cloud backup is turned off for ShotSmart, so this data is not copied to your Google account backup.

Actions that leave the app through your choice

Some actions hand information to other apps that you choose. ShotSmart does not see what those apps do next.

  • Calendar. Opens your calendar app with an event that has the date, time, address and text filled in.
  • Maps. Opens the address in your map app.
  • Phone, messages and contacts. Opens the dialer, a new message or a new contact with the number or email filled in. Nothing is dialled, sent or saved until you confirm in that app.
  • Email. Opens your email app with a draft. Reporting bad text recognition opens a draft with the report text.
  • Links and translation. Opens the link in your browser, or opens the translation page with the text you chose.
  • Share and export. Shares text or an image through Android's share sheet, or exports your history as a JSON file to a location you choose. The file is created on your phone and leaves only when you share it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ShotSmart shows a banner on the home screen and offers an optional rewarded video that removes the banner for 24 hours. These ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use the advertising ID of your device (the AD_ID permission) and request ads over the internet. The developer receives no advertising data about you.

  • Consent. Before any ad is requested, ShotSmart asks Google's User Messaging Platform for consent where it is required, for example in the EEA and the UK. Ads are requested only when that consent allows it.
  • Privacy options. If your region requires it, Settings > Remove ads shows a "Privacy options" button to change your consent choice at any time.
  • Google's terms. Google's handling of ad data is described in its privacy policy and its ads technologies page.

Subscription (Google Play Billing)

ShotSmart offers an optional monthly subscription that removes the banner. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. ShotSmart receives only whether your subscription is active. It does not receive your payment details, and it does not keep a copy of your Google account.

Internet access

ShotSmart uses the internet for ad requests, consent checks and the subscription check described above. It does not upload your images, text or history.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Ads, consent and subscription checks.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID). Used by the ad SDK, as described above.

ShotSmart does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, storage or notification access. Calendar, contacts and messages are used only through the apps you pick.

Data deletion

You can delete a single analysis from its screen, or delete everything from Settings > Delete all history & images. This removes the images, thumbnails and database records from your phone. Uninstalling ShotSmart also removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us. Files you shared or exported are yours; delete them in the app you sent them to.

Children

ShotSmart is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
