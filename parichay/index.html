Parichay Privacy Policy

App: Parichay (parichay) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

Parichay is a marriage biodata maker for Android. It works entirely on your device: there is no account, no sign-in, no developer server, and no advertising or analytics SDK. It declares no INTERNET permission, so it cannot send your biodata or photos anywhere on its own.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Biodata profiles you create: the personal, education, family, horoscope and contact details you type, together with the template and document language chosen for each profile.
  • A private copy of each photo you add, including the cropped version used by a template. The original in your gallery is not modified.
  • Settings such as the light, dark or system theme.

Profiles live in one JSON file and the photo copies in a separate folder in the app's private storage. Deleting a profile removes its photo copies, and Settings > Delete all data removes every profile and photo. Android's automatic backup is turned off for Parichay (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

Parichay does not add its own encryption to these files; they rely on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

The system photo picker gives Parichay read access only to the picture you choose. The app copies that picture into its private storage; it does not scan or list your gallery.

When you share, the PDF or JPEG is written to the app's cache and handed to Android's share sheet as a read-only FileProvider URI. You can also save the PDF through the system document picker directly to a folder you choose. A PDF saved that way is outside the app and remains after you uninstall Parichay until you delete it. An app you share to keeps its own copy under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Ads, accounts and purchases

Parichay has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

Parichay is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Parichay deletes the app's private storage, including every saved profile and all photo copies. You can also clear it from Android Settings > Apps > Parichay > Storage > Clear storage, or use Settings > Delete all data inside the app. PDFs and JPEGs you saved or shared outside the app are not deleted by uninstalling; remove them where you saved them, or in the app that received them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
