Northly Privacy Policy

App: Northly: Compass & Altimeter (com.coderhub.northly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Northly is a compass and altimeter that works offline. It shows true and magnetic north, the Qibla direction, altitude from GPS and from the barometer, coordinates, sun and moon times, and a pressure trend. Your location is used on your phone only, and the developer does not receive it. Northly has no accounts and no analytics SDK. It shows banner and rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, and it offers an optional subscription through Google Play.

Location

  • When it is used. Northly asks for precise and approximate location. Location is requested only while an instrument screen is open (for example the compass, altitude, sun and moon or waypoints screen). Northly never uses location in the background.
  • What it is used for. GPS altitude, coordinates, the Qibla direction, the magnetic declination for true north, sunrise and sunset times, and the distance and bearing to a saved waypoint.
  • Not uploaded. Northly does not send your location, altitude or headings to the developer or to any server. Magnetic declination and the sun and moon times are calculated on your phone, with no network request.
  • Waypoints. When you save a waypoint, its name, coordinates, altitude (if known) and time are stored in the app's private database on your phone. Waypoints are not sent anywhere.

Sensors

The compass uses your phone's magnetometer and motion sensors, and the altimeter uses its barometer. These sensors need no permission. Northly reads them only while the screen that uses them is open, and stops reading when the app is paused. Barometer readings used for the pressure trend are kept in memory for up to six hours and are cleared when the app closes. They are not saved to storage.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Banner. A small banner may appear on the compass screen. Rewarded videos can be started from Settings. Both are provided by Google AdMob.
  • Consent. Before any ad is requested, Northly asks Google's User Messaging Platform for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK). Ads are requested only when that consent allows it. If consent cannot be checked, no ads are requested.
  • Privacy options. Where required, Settings shows a Privacy options entry that lets you review or change your consent choice at any time.
  • What Google receives. AdMob may collect device and usage information, including an advertising identifier, to show and measure ads, under Google's privacy policy. Northly does not send Google your location, altitude, headings or waypoints.
  • Rewarded video. Watching a video gives 24 hours without the banner. Nothing else is unlocked by ads.

Subscription (Google Play)

An optional subscription removes the banner. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing, under Google's terms. Northly receives only the purchase status from Google Play. It does not receive your payment details.

Information stored on your device

  • Your settings: units, north reference, declination mode and value, coordinate format, altimeter pressure (QNH), theme, night red mode and subscription and ad status. They are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Your saved waypoints, in the app's private database.
  • Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Northly, so these items are not copied to your Google account.

Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate). Described above.
  • Internet and network state. Used for Google AdMob ads, the consent form and Google Play Billing. Northly does not send your measurements over the network.
  • Advertising ID. Declared for AdMob ads.

Data deletion

Settings > Data > Delete all data removes your waypoints, stored samples and settings from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us. Data held by Google (for example ad and purchase records) is handled under Google's own policies.

Not for navigation

Northly is not a certified navigation, marine or aviation instrument. Do not rely on it for safety-critical decisions.

Children

Northly is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
