FocusWindow Privacy Policy

App: FocusWindow (com.coderhub.focuswindow) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

FocusWindow is a focus timer. You write an intention, choose a session length, and the app keeps your sessions, notes, daily budgets and manual time notes on your phone. It can also show per-app screen time from Android's own usage records, but only if you turn that on. The developer does not run any server for FocusWindow, does not receive your data, and does not sell or share it. FocusWindow has no accounts, no ads, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no tracking.

What FocusWindow stores on your phone

  • Focus sessions. The intention, planned length, start and end times, paused time, outcome (completed or ended early), the app names you listed as planned, and any note you write.
  • Budgets. The app or activity name, a daily limit in minutes, and whether it is switched on.
  • Manual time. Entries you type in (a name and a number of minutes) for days or apps you do not measure automatically.
  • Settings. Your theme choice, the haptic switch, and the time when usage access was last granted or the usage history was last cleared.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. It is not copied to any other place by the app.

Usage access (optional)

FocusWindow asks you to turn on Usage access in Android settings. This is the PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS permission. It is optional. With it, FocusWindow reads Android's own records of which app was in the foreground and for how long, for the last seven days, and shows daily and weekly totals per app. Without it, the focus timer, sessions, notes, budgets and manual time all work.

  • FocusWindow reads only app names and foreground start and stop times. It never reads screen content, messages, notifications, keystrokes, photos, contacts or location.
  • FocusWindow does not block, close or interrupt any app.
  • Days before access was granted are not shown as measured. Days with missing records say so.
  • You can review or revoke the permission at any time in Android settings. Denying it does not end a focus session.

Widget and Quick Settings tile

The home-screen widget shows today's focused time, the number of completed sessions and your streak, using the data above. Tapping the widget or the Quick Settings tile opens the focus setup. Neither one sends any data anywhere.

Export and deletion

  • Export. When you tap Export local JSON, FocusWindow prepares a file with your sessions, budgets, manual entries and the last seven days of usage totals, then opens Android's share sheet. The app does not send it. What happens to the file after you share it is up to the app or service you choose.
  • Delete usage history. Removes manual entries and sets a new start point for measured time. Android keeps its own usage records; FocusWindow cannot delete those, and the app says so.
  • Delete all FocusWindow data. Removes sessions, notes, budgets, manual entries and the local ledger from the phone. Your theme and haptic choices stay.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its private data.

Backup

FocusWindow turns off Android's automatic backup and device-to-device transfer for its app data, so your sessions and notes are not uploaded to a Google account backup. FocusWindow has no backup feature of its own.

Network

FocusWindow does not request internet access. It makes no network requests and sends no data off the device.

Permissions

  • Usage access (optional). Described above.
  • Vibration. Not requested. The haptic pulse when a session closes uses the system haptic feedback, which needs no permission.

Children

FocusWindow is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. It is not a parental-control product and does not report to anyone.
