Hush Privacy Policy

App: Hush: White Noise & Sleep (com.coderhub.hush) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Hush makes white noise and sleep sounds on your phone. Every sound is generated on the device while it plays. Hush does not download or stream audio. It has no account, no sign-in and no analytics, and it does not use the microphone. Your mixes, timers, wake time and listening history stay on your phone. The developer does not run a server for Hush and does not receive your data. Hush shows ads in its free version: a small banner on the home screen during the day, and rewarded videos that you choose to watch. Ads are never shown while sound is playing or between 21:00 and 08:00 local time.

Sound generation and playback

  • All sounds are computed on your device by the app's own code. Nothing is downloaded or streamed to play sound.
  • Playback runs in a foreground service with a media notification, so it continues with the screen off. The notification and the lock screen show play, pause and stop controls.
  • Hush pauses when headphones are unplugged, for phone calls and for other apps that take audio focus, and resumes after a temporary interruption when it was playing.

Information stored on your device

  • Saved mixes. The sounds, volumes, timer and fade you save with a mix name.
  • Listening history. The start time and length of your recent sessions. It is kept in the app's private database, keeps the last 30 sessions, and is never uploaded.
  • Settings. Theme, red mode, dim overlay, master volume, last mix, sleep timer, fade length, wake-up time, and your ad choices.
  • Ad and subscription status. Whether you have removed ads by watching a video or by subscribing, and the ad consent answer from Google's consent tool.

Settings > Data > Delete all data removes the mixes, history and settings from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes its data.

Wake-up alarm

If you set a wake-up time, Hush asks Android to start it at that time. The alarm is stored on your phone. After a restart Hush sets the alarm again so that it still rings. Hush uses the alarm to start the last mix; it does not read anything else.

Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile

The widget and the tile start, pause or resume your last mix. They read only the playback state inside the app, and they send nothing outside your phone.

Ads

  • Google AdMob. The free version shows a banner on the home screen between 08:00 and 21:00, when no sound is playing, and a rewarded video you can choose to watch in Settings. Google's SDK loads these ads, so it receives your IP address, device information and an advertising identifier. Ads are personalised only if Google's consent tool allows it.
  • Consent. Before any ad loads, Hush asks Google's User Messaging Platform whether ads may be requested. If you are in a region that requires it, Settings > Privacy > Privacy options lets you change your choice at any time. If consent is not given, no ads are loaded.
  • Google's terms. Google handles ad data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalisation in Android settings.

Subscription

Removing the banner is available as a subscription through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment and tells Hush whether the subscription is active. Hush does not receive your payment details or your Google account.

Permissions

  • Foreground service and media playback. Keeps sound playing with the screen off.
  • Notifications. Shows the playback controls and the wake-up alarm. Hush asks for this permission the first time you start playback on Android 13 and later.
  • Run at startup. Sets the wake-up alarm again after a restart.
  • Internet and network state. Used only by the ad and subscription components described above. The sound engine makes no network requests.
  • Advertising ID. Used by Google's ad SDK in the free version.

Hush does not request the microphone, location, contacts, photos or camera.

Health

Hush is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or cure insomnia or any other condition.

Children

Hush is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
