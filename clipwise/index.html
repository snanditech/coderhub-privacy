Clipwise Privacy Policy

App: Clipwise (com.coderhub.clipwise) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Clipwise cuts short clips from long videos on your phone. Trimming, captions you type or paste, post text and export all run on your device. The developer does not run a server for Clipwise, does not receive your videos, and does not sell or share your data. Clipwise has no accounts and no analytics SDK. It does show ads (see "Ads" below) and it sells an optional subscription through Google Play.

Videos you import

  • How you pick a video. You choose a video with Android's photo picker. Clipwise does not ask for storage or media permissions. Android may give Clipwise read access to that one video. Clipwise keeps the reference to it in the app's private database so you can continue a project.
  • Processing. Trimming and export run on your phone. Each clip is re-encoded with Media3 and saved to the app's private folder. Videos are never uploaded by Clipwise.
  • Sharing. A clip or caption file is shared only when you tap Share. Android then gives the app you choose access to that one file.

Captions and post text

  • Captions from text. If you paste the words spoken in a clip, Clipwise times them across the clip on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Post text. The limits check, hook suggestion and copy or share actions run on your phone. Post text is not saved with the project.

Optional AI suggestions (your own endpoint)

AI suggestions are off until you add an endpoint and API key in Settings. The endpoint must use https. When you run AI analysis, Clipwise sends the length of the video and your API key (in the request header) to the endpoint address you entered. It does not send the video file. The endpoint you choose handles the request under its own privacy policy. The developer does not receive these requests.

  • Key storage. Your API key is encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore. The key is not in the backup, because Android backup is turned off for Clipwise.
  • Results. AI output is labelled as generated and can be wrong. "Report this result" opens an email to the developer with the clip times, score and caption text. No video is included. The email is sent only when you tap Send in your mail app.

Ads and consent

  • Banner ads (free version). The home screen can show a small Google AdMob banner. Rewarded videos can remove the banner for 24 hours.
  • Consent. Before any ad loads, Clipwise asks Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. Ads load only when UMP says ads may be requested. If consent cannot be checked, no ads load.
  • Ad data. AdMob processes ad requests under Google's privacy policy. It may use your advertising ID (Android permission AD_ID) and device information to show and measure ads. Where UMP requires it, you can change your choices in Settings > Ads privacy > Privacy options.

Subscription

The optional Pro subscription is processed by Google Play Billing. Clipwise receives only whether your subscription is active. Pro removes the banner ads. Manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play.

Internet use

Clipwise uses the internet for three things only: the ad services above, Google Play Billing, and the AI endpoint you configure. Trimming, captions, export and sharing work without a connection.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Ads, billing and your AI endpoint.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID). Used by AdMob for the ads described above.

Clipwise does not request the camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions.

Data stored on your device

  • Projects, source video references, clips, captions and export records, kept in the app's private database.
  • Exported MP4 clips and caption (.srt) files, kept in the app's private folder.
  • Settings, including theme, ad-free status, and the AI endpoint address and encrypted key.

Data deletion

Settings > Data > Delete all data removes projects, clips, captions, exported files and settings. Deleting a project removes its clips, captions and exported files. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data. Clips you shared, and any email you sent, are held by the apps and services you used. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Clipwise is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
