Bubbly Privacy Policy

App: Bubbly (bubbly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

Bubbly is an offline single-player bubble shooter. It keeps your current board, campaign progress, daily challenge and local statistics on this device. It has no account, no developer server and no INTERNET permission. There are no online rooms, leaderboards, cloud saves, ads, analytics or purchases.

What the app stores on your device

Bubbly keeps two JSON files in its private storage under files/bubbly:

  • game.json: the current board and shot state, including mode, level, daily date, rows and columns, bubble colours and positions, current and next bubble colours, score, moves left, combo, game status, generator seed, random-number state, pending booster and bubbles popped.
  • stats.json: best score, number of new levels cleared, highest level reached, total bubbles popped, booster inventory, all-time daily best, last daily date, daily streak and games played.

Private app preferences store the theme, whether colour-blind patterns are shown, and whether haptics are enabled. A short-lived .tmp file is used while saving.

Bubbly does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera or any account. The board and statistics are game data, not a profile.

The files are not separately encrypted by Bubbly. They rely on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves the device

Nothing is uploaded or shared. Bubbly declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, advertising, analytics or billing SDK. It has no share or export feature. Campaign boards are generated locally from a seed; no level, score or identifier is sent to a server.

Permissions

Bubbly declares no Android runtime permission. It does not request storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, notification access or an exact alarm. Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION; it is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Game content

Boards are generated by app code. Bubbles and colour-blind patterns are drawn by the app; no third-party level packs, music, sound recordings or licensed artwork are bundled.

Daily board and local progress

The daily challenge is derived from the device's local calendar date. Changing the device date or time zone can change which daily board appears and how a streak is counted. Campaign progress, booster inventory, the current board and statistics are local to this installation.

Campaign boards are generated on the device. Difficulty varies, and a particular generated board is not guaranteed to be winnable; no board or score is verified by a server. The undo history is kept in memory for the current session and is not saved after the app is closed.

Android backup and device transfer

Android automatic backup is turned off for Bubbly: the manifest sets allowBackup=false and fullBackupContent=false. The app's extraction rules also exclude the database and sharedpref domains from cloud backup, but they do not include a separate device-transfer block and do not name the files/bubbly records individually. The manifest switch is the primary control.

This policy does not claim that a manufacturer's device-to-device transfer tool, a rooted device or a forensic copy cannot access app data. To remove the app's data reliably, clear its storage in Android settings or uninstall it. If backup behaviour is ever re-enabled, the extraction rules should be widened before this page can claim that app files are excluded.

Deletion

Bubbly has no in-app button that resets all statistics. “Start a new board” discards the current board but leaves campaign progress and statistics in place. If the saved board is unreadable, the recovery option can discard it without resetting statistics.

To delete all Bubbly data, clear the app's storage from Android Settings > Apps > Bubbly > Storage, or uninstall the app. There is no developer server copy to delete, and no shared score or board copy outside the app.

Ads, accounts and purchases

Bubbly has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no paid boosters and no energy timers. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

Bubbly is a casual puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts, chat or online features and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
