ResumeKit Privacy Policy

App: ResumeKit (resumekit) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

ResumeKit is a resume and cover-letter builder for Android. Editing, layout and PDF generation happen on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no AI service, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Resumes and cover letters you create, including the name, contact details, work history, education, skills, projects, certifications and any other text you type.
  • Named versions of a resume and their created, updated and last-exported dates.
  • Text you import from a .txt or .md file, after it has been parsed into sections.
  • Settings such as the theme.

Everything lives in one JSON workspace file under filesDir/resumekit plus private preferences. Android's automatic backup is turned off for ResumeKit (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not back up the workspace to Google Drive.

ResumeKit does not add its own encryption to the workspace; it relies on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. ResumeKit declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, and it has no AI wording service, so no resume text is sent anywhere.

PDFs and plain-text files are written to the app's cache and shared through Android's share sheet using a read-only FileProvider URI. Imported .txt/.md files are read through the system document picker with access only to the file you choose. PDF import is not supported.

Backup and restore is manual. When you choose Save backup, ResumeKit writes one JSON file through the system document picker to the location you choose. That file contains the whole workspace, is not uploaded, and stays where you save it after uninstall. Restoring replaces the current workspace after a confirmation.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Sensitive information

A resume usually contains personal contact details and employment history, and may mention other people such as references. Treat the workspace and any backup file as sensitive: they can be read by anyone who can unlock the device or open the backup file.

Ads, accounts and purchases

ResumeKit has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

ResumeKit is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling ResumeKit deletes the workspace and settings with the app's private storage. You can also delete resumes and versions inside the app, or clear everything from Android Settings > Apps > ResumeKit > Storage > Clear storage. Manual backup files, PDFs and text exports are outside the app and are not deleted by uninstalling; remove them where you saved them or in the app that received them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
