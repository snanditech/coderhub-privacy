StaffDay Privacy Policy

App: StaffDay (staffday) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

StaffDay keeps staff attendance, overtime, advances and monthly salary for a small employer. Payroll arithmetic, salary slips and CSV files are produced on the phone. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • Staff records: name, role, monthly salary, joining date, active or inactive status and an optional phone number.
  • Attendance: present, half day, absent or paid leave for each staff member and date, plus overtime minutes.
  • Advances and payments: amount, date and note, with part payments against a month's salary.
  • Settings such as theme and the employer name printed on salary slips.

Roster, attendance, advances and payments are stored in one JSON file in the app's private storage; settings are private preferences. Android's automatic backup is turned off for StaffDay (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

StaffDay does not add its own encryption to its records; they rely on Android's app sandbox and the encryption Android applies to device storage.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

Salary slips and payroll CSV files are written to the app's cache and handed to Android's share sheet as read-only FileProvider URIs. They contain staff names, attendance, salary, advances and payments. The cache copies are temporary, but an app you share them to keeps its own copy under its own policy. There is no print or save-to-folder feature.

Employment records

Staff names, phone numbers, salary and attendance are personal information about other people. They stay on this phone and are not sent to the developer. StaffDay is a record keeper: it does not pay salaries, file statutory returns or claim to handle PF, ESI or TDS. You are responsible for who can unlock the phone and open the app, and the app adds no separate login or database encryption.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android runtime permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's own signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Ads, accounts and purchases

StaffDay has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

StaffDay is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling StaffDay deletes its roster, attendance, advances, payments and settings. You can also delete staff and payments in the app, or clear everything from Android Settings > Apps > StaffDay > Storage > Clear storage. Salary slips and CSVs you shared are outside the app and remain until you delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
