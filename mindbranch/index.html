MindBranch Privacy Policy

App: MindBranch – Mind Maps (com.coderhub.mindbranch) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

MindBranch is an app for drawing mind maps: ideas as branches, with notes, colours, symbols, crosslinks and an outline view. Your maps are stored only on your device. The developer does not run any server for MindBranch, does not receive your maps, notes or any other data, and does not sell or share data. MindBranch has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Network access

MindBranch does not request the internet permission and makes no network requests. Nothing you write or draw leaves the device unless you export it yourself and send or share the file.

Information stored on your device

  • Your maps. Titles, node labels, notes, colours, symbols, positions, collapsed state and crosslinks are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Every change is saved straight away, so the app reopens where you left it.
  • Settings. Your choice of light, dark or system appearance is saved in the same private storage.
  • Recovery copy. If the saved library is damaged, the app keeps the damaged data in private storage so it can be recovered, and starts with a starter map.

The app does not read your contacts, calendar, photos, files, location or other apps' data. It uses no device identifiers.

Files you export or import

  • Export. You choose where to save a PNG image, a PDF, a JSON backup, a Markdown outline or an OPML outline, using Android's file picker. The file is written only to the place you choose. You can also copy JSON or Markdown to the clipboard. What happens to a file after that is up to you and the app you share it with.
  • Import. You choose a JSON backup or a Markdown outline with Android's file picker. The app reads only that file and adds the map to your library on the device.

Backups by Android

Android's automatic backup is turned off for MindBranch, so your maps are not uploaded to your Google account backup. Use the JSON export if you want a copy you control.

Permissions

MindBranch requests no permissions beyond what Android grants to every app. It does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage access or notifications.

Data deletion

MindBranch keeps its data only in its private storage on your device. To remove everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android's app settings. Files you exported are separate files on your device or in your cloud app; delete them there. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

MindBranch is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
