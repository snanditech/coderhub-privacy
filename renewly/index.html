Renewly Privacy Policy

App: Renewly – Subscription & Bill Tracker (com.coderhub.renewly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Renewly is a tracker for subscriptions and recurring bills. You type in your subscriptions, and Renewly keeps them on your phone and shows the monthly and yearly cost and the renewal dates. The developer does not run any server for Renewly, does not receive your subscription data, and does not sell or share it. Renewly has no accounts and no analytics SDK. It shows advertising through Google AdMob, and it offers an optional monthly subscription through Google Play.

Information stored on your device

  • Subscriptions. The name, amount, currency, billing cycle, next due date, reminder setting, active or paused status and notes you enter. They are stored in a database in the app's private storage.
  • Settings. Theme, dynamic colour, default currency, onboarding status and the state of your ad-free period or subscription, kept in the app's private settings storage.
  • Reminders. Renewly schedules a daily background check on your phone. When a renewal is due on the reminder day you chose, it shows a notification. Reminders are generated on your phone and are not sent anywhere.

Uninstalling Renewly removes this data. Renewly's backup setting is off, so the data is not included in Android's automatic cloud backup.

CSV export

Settings > Export subscriptions (CSV) writes a text file of your subscriptions to a location you choose with Android's file picker. The file is created only when you ask for it. Renewly does not upload it. Once saved, the file is under your control, and what happens to it depends on where you save it or what you share it with.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Renewly shows a banner ad on its home screen, and it offers a rewarded video that turns the banner off for 24 hours. To show ads, Google's AdMob SDK requests ads from Google's servers. These requests can include your IP address, an advertising identifier and basic device and app information. Renewly does not send your subscriptions, amounts or notes to Google.

  • Consent. Before any ad is requested, Renewly uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show the consent form that applies in your region. Renewly requests ads only when UMP reports that ads can be requested. If the consent check fails, Renewly shows no ads.
  • Privacy options. When Google reports that privacy options are required in your region, Settings > Ads and privacy > Privacy options opens the form so you can change your choice.
  • Google's terms. Google handles ad requests under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy, and how Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services.

Subscription (Google Play Billing)

An optional Pro subscription hides the home-screen banner. Purchases go through Google Play Billing, and Google processes the payment under its own terms. Renewly receives only whether a subscription is active. It does not receive your payment details or your Google account information.

Internet use

Renewly does not need the internet for its core features. The internet is used only for ads (AdMob) and for checking the Pro subscription with Google Play.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Ads and the Pro subscription check.
  • Advertising ID. Used by AdMob for ads. You can reset or delete it in your Android settings.
  • Notifications. Renewal reminders. Renewly asks for this permission only when you turn a reminder on. If you refuse, reminders are saved but no notification is shown.

Renewly does not request your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or SMS messages.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes all subscriptions and settings from the app. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data. Google's ad and purchase records are held by Google under its own policies. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Renewly is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
