Carromly Privacy Policy

App: Carromly: Offline Carrom (com.coderhub.carromly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Carromly is an offline carrom board game for one or two players. The game does not collect personal information, does not connect to the internet, and does not send anything from your phone to the developer or to anyone else. Carromly has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics. The only things it keeps are your game settings and the game in progress, and those stay on your phone.

Internet, location, camera and microphone

  • Internet. Carromly does not request the internet permission and makes no network requests.
  • Location. Carromly does not ask for or use your location.
  • Camera and microphone. Carromly does not use the camera or the microphone.

Information stored on your device

  • Settings. Your theme choice (follow system, light or dark), the Sound switch and the Vibration switch. These are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Game in progress. The board, the scores, whose turn it is, the match length and the computer's difficulty level, so that you can resume a game you left half way through. It is saved after each settled shot and removed when a game is won.

Nothing else is stored: there is no record of your games over time, no names and no identifiers.

Android backup

Carromly does not have its own cloud backup. Android's automatic backup for apps may include the app's private storage, which means your settings and the game in progress could be included in a backup of your phone, governed by the backup settings in your Google account. The developer does not receive these backups and cannot read them. You can turn automatic backup off in your phone's settings.

Sound and vibration

Carromly plays a short system sound and a short vibration on pots, fouls, queen moves and wins, if you have those switched on in Settings. Nothing about these is recorded or sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Vibrate. Used for the short vibration described above. You can turn it off in Settings.

Carromly declares no other permissions.

Data deletion

Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall Carromly, to remove its settings and the saved game. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Carromly is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
