TrueLevel Privacy Policy

App: TrueLevel – Bubble Level & Ruler (com.coderhub.truelevel) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

TrueLevel is a bubble level, surface and angle meter, and on-screen ruler. It works offline. Your readings, calibration and settings stay on your phone. TrueLevel has no account, no analytics and no sign-in, and the developer does not run a server that receives your data. The home screen shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. Google's ad SDK can collect device and advertising data, as described below.

What the app does with your data

  • Sensor readings. The level, surface and angle screens read the phone's motion sensors (accelerometer) while the screen is open. Readings are used on the screen only and are not sent anywhere.
  • Calibration and settings. Zero points you set, the ruler calibration factor, units, tolerance, sound, haptic, night mode and keep-screen-on settings are stored on your phone in the app's private storage.
  • Saved calibration history. Each calibration you make is kept on your phone so you can see when it was made. You can delete all of it in Settings > Delete all data.
  • Sharing. When you tap Share reading, the app passes a short text line (for example, the level deviation or angle) to the sharing app you choose. The app does not add your name, location or device identifiers to it.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts. TrueLevel does not ask for these permissions.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

TrueLevel shows a banner ad on the home screen and offers an optional rewarded video. These ads are served by Google AdMob. Before any ad is loaded, the app asks Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for your consent where the law requires it, and it shows ads only when UMP allows that. If you refuse, or consent cannot be checked, no ads are loaded.

  • Google may collect an advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID), your IP address and information about your device and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. TrueLevel declares the advertising ID permission for this purpose.
  • Ads are delivered over the internet, so TrueLevel uses the network while ads are shown. The rest of the app works without a connection.
  • Google handles this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services.
  • You can remove the banner. Watching a rewarded video turns the banner off for 24 hours. A Pro subscription removes it for as long as the subscription is active.

Purchases

The Pro subscription is sold and managed by Google Play Billing. TrueLevel receives only whether your subscription is active. It does not receive your name, payment details or billing address. You can manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Needed to load ads and to check Play Billing. Measuring does not use the network.
  • Advertising ID. Used by Google AdMob, as described above.

TrueLevel does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or notification permissions.

Backups

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for TrueLevel. Your calibration and settings stay on the phone where you made them.

Children

TrueLevel is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may show ads to children according to its own policies.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes your calibration history, saved measurements and settings from the phone. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the phone. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us. To control ad data, use your Google account's ad settings and the ad consent options offered in the app.

Accuracy

TrueLevel is a pocket reference, not a certified instrument. Phone sensors have limits and drift. For critical work, use a calibrated tool.
