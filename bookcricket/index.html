Book Cricket Privacy Policy

App: Book Cricket (bookcricket) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

Book Cricket is the playground cricket game rebuilt for one phone. Every rule, page flip and score is handled on the device. It has no account, no sign-in, no developer server, no online play, no advertising or analytics SDK, and it declares no INTERNET permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on this phone:

  • The match in progress, including team names you enter, innings, wickets and the random sequence needed to resume.
  • History of the last 100 matches, with team names, scores and results.
  • The daily challenge board and streak.
  • Settings such as theme, default overs and wickets, haptics and keep-screen-on.

These are JSON files and private preferences in the app's storage. Android's automatic backup is turned off for Book Cricket (the manifest sets allowBackup=false), so Android does not copy this data to Google Drive.

What leaves your device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no network code, so it cannot send your content to the developer, an ad network or anyone else.

You can share a finished result as plain text through Android's share sheet. The receiving app keeps its own copy under its own policy. The daily challenge seed is computed locally from the calendar date, not fetched from a server.

Permissions

None. The app declares no Android permission. It does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notification access.

Ads, accounts and purchases

Book Cricket has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads and no in-app purchases, and the developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

Book Cricket is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

Uninstalling Book Cricket deletes the saved match, match history, daily board and settings with the app's private storage. You can also clear them from Android Settings > Apps > Book Cricket > Storage > Clear storage. Shared result text is outside the app and remains with the app that received it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
