ScanBee Privacy Policy

App: ScanBee – QR & Barcode Scanner (com.coderhub.scanbee) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

ScanBee reads QR codes and barcodes with your camera or from a photo you choose, and creates QR codes and barcodes. Scanning, creating, history and export all happen on your phone. The developer does not run any server for ScanBee, does not receive the codes you scan or create, and does not sell or share your data. ScanBee has no account and no analytics SDK. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and it offers an optional Pro subscription through Google Play.

Camera

  • When it is used. ScanBee asks for the camera permission the first time you open the Scan screen. The camera is used only while a scan screen is open to read codes. Frames are analysed on your phone and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Without the permission. You can still scan a code from a photo, create codes and read your history.

Photos you scan

To scan a code from a picture, ScanBee uses Android's photo picker. The picker gives ScanBee access only to the photo you choose. ScanBee does not request access to your photo library. The photo is read into memory, the code is decoded on your phone, and the picture itself is not stored by ScanBee. Only the decoded text of the code is saved in your history.

Logos in codes you create

If you add a logo to a QR code, ScanBee reads the image you choose through the photo picker and draws it into the code on your phone. The logo is not saved in your history.

Scan history and created codes

  • Stored on your phone. ScanBee keeps a history of scanned codes and of codes you created, in a database in the app's private storage. This includes the decoded text, the detected type, the time, favourites, tags and notes you add.
  • Search and export. Search runs on your phone. When you export history or a batch as CSV, ScanBee writes a file to its private cache and opens Android's share sheet so you choose where the file goes. What happens to the file after that is up to the app or service you pick.
  • Deletion. You can delete single items, or use Settings > Delete all app data to remove the history, the batch sessions and the saved settings.

Link checks

Before a link opens, ScanBee shows the full address and runs structural checks on your phone. These checks look at the scheme, the host name, IP addresses, shorteners, look-alike brand names and similar patterns. ScanBee does not visit the link, does not follow redirects, does not fetch the page and does not scan for malware. No information about the link is sent to the developer or to any service.

Ads (Google AdMob and consent)

  • What is shown. A banner on the home screen and an optional rewarded video in Settings. Ads are not shown on the live scan view or on the result screen.
  • Google AdMob. The ads are served by Google AdMob. AdMob receives technical information needed to show an ad, such as your IP address, the device's advertising ID, the ad request and the app version. Google uses this under its own policies: Google privacy policy.
  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions that require it, ScanBee uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent before any ad is requested. If consent is not given, or is not yet known, ads are not requested. You can change your choice later through the privacy options offered by the app.
  • Advertising ID. ScanBee declares the advertising ID permission because AdMob uses it. ScanBee does not read or store the advertising ID itself.

Pro subscription (Google Play Billing)

The optional Pro subscription is sold and managed by Google Play. Google Play processes the payment and tells ScanBee whether a subscription is active. ScanBee stores only that status on your phone. It does not receive your name, payment details or Google account information. Pro removes the home-screen banner.

Network access

ScanBee needs the internet permission only for ads and for the Google Play subscription check. Scanning, creating codes, link checks, history and export do not use the network.

Permissions

  • Camera. Reads codes while a scan screen is open.
  • Internet and network state. Ads and the subscription check described above.
  • Advertising ID. Used by AdMob to serve ads.

ScanBee does not request location, contacts, microphone, storage or media library access, and it does not use Android's cloud backup.

Information stored on your device

  • Your scan and created-code history, batch sessions, favourites, tags and notes, in the app's private database.
  • Settings such as theme, scan preferences, default code options and Pro status, in the app's private storage.
  • Temporary export files, in the app's cache. They are removed after a day.
  • Android's cloud backup is turned off for ScanBee, so the data does not leave the device through it.

Children

ScanBee is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
