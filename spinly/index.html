Spinly Privacy Policy

App: Spinly – Random Picker & Dice (com.coderhub.spinly) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026 · Contact: technicalexplorer20@gmail.com

Summary

Spinly rolls dice, flips coins, picks numbers, draws names, splits teams, spins a wheel and draws cards. Your lists, wheels and history are kept on your phone. The developer does not run a server for Spinly, has no accounts, and does not collect or sell your personal data. Spinly shows a banner ad on its home screen, provided by Google AdMob, and offers an optional subscription that removes it.

Information kept on your device

  • History. Each result (its time, the dice or number settings and the outcome) is saved in a database in the app's private storage. You can delete single entries or all of them in History.
  • Saved lists and wheels. Names, weights and wheel options you save are kept in the same private storage.
  • Settings. Theme, Secure RNG, sound and haptic switches and similar choices are kept in the app's private storage.

Nothing in this list is uploaded by Spinly. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Settings > Data > Delete all data removes history, saved lists, wheels and settings at once.

Randomness

Spinly makes every draw on your device. It does not send your choices, names or results anywhere. Secure RNG in Settings switches draws to Android's cryptographic random number generator.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The home screen shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. Results, dice, coins, numbers, lists, teams, wheels and cards never show an ad.
  • Before any ad loads, Spinly asks Google's User Messaging Platform whether ads may be requested. Where a consent form is required, you see it first. If consent is not given, no ad loads.
  • To remove the banner for 24 hours, you can watch a rewarded video ad. Google AdMob handles the ad and may use your device's advertising ID (the Android AD_ID permission) and IP address, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Spinly uses Google's test ad units in this version. Live ad units will be set before release.

Subscription (Google Play Billing)

The optional monthly subscription, which removes the banner, is sold through Google Play. Google Play handles payment and tells Spinly whether a subscription is active. Spinly keeps only a yes-or-no entitlement on your device. The developer does not see your payment details.

Network use

Spinly needs the internet only for the ads and the subscription check described above. All other features work in airplane mode. Spinly requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or notification permission.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state. Ads and Google Play Billing.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID). Used by Google AdMob, as described above.

Sharing

When you tap Share result, Spinly opens Android's share sheet with the result as plain text. The app you choose receives that text. Spinly does not send it anywhere itself.

Children

Spinly is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
