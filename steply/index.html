Steply Privacy Policy

App: Steply: Step Counter (com.coderhub.steply) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

Steply is a step counter. It reads the step sensor built into your phone, counts your steps, and keeps the totals on your phone. It does not use GPS or your location, it has no account, and the developer does not run any server that receives your step data. Steply does not collect, sell or share your step data.

Steply shows ads. Ads come from Google AdMob, which uses the internet and may use an advertising ID under Google's own policies. Section "Ads and consent" below explains this.

Step data

  • What is read. Steply reads the cumulative step count from the phone's step counter sensor (or the step detector sensor if the phone has no counter). Steply does not read your activity history from other apps.
  • What is stored. The daily step total, distance and active minutes estimates, your daily goal, and an hourly breakdown for today. These are stored in a database in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Where it goes. Nowhere. Step data is not sent to the developer, to Google or to any other party by Steply.
  • Reboots. The step counter restarts at zero when the phone restarts. Steply keeps the steps already saved for the day and adds the steps counted since the restart. Steply stores the reading it needs for this on your phone.

Location

Steply does not request location permission and does not collect your location in any form. It does not add location to your files.

Distance and calories

Distance and calories are estimates calculated on your phone from your steps and the height, weight and stride length you enter, if you enter them. They are not measurements. Height and weight are stored only on your phone and are not sent anywhere.

Notifications

Steply can show an ongoing notification with today's step count while it counts in the background. Notifications are optional. You can turn them off in Android's settings for Steply, and Steply still counts steps without them. Steply does not send push notifications from a server.

Home-screen widget

The widget shows today's steps and goal progress from data stored on your phone. It does not send any data.

Export

When you choose Export steps as CSV in Settings, Steply creates a file with one row per day and saves it to the place you pick with Android's file picker. Steply does not upload the file. What happens to the file after you save it is up to you and any app you share it with.

Ads and consent

  • Google AdMob. The home-screen banner and the optional rewarded video are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob requests use the internet and may use your advertising ID, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Consent. Before Steply requests ads, it uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it. Steply requests ads only when that consent allows it. If consent is not given or cannot be checked, Steply shows no ads.
  • Change your choices. If the consent form applies to you, Settings shows a Privacy options entry where you can change your choices at any time.
  • Hiding ads. You can watch a short rewarded video to hide the banner for 24 hours. Steply does not show ads on the Today screen when you are subscribed.

Purchases

The optional subscription that hides the banner is handled by Google Play Billing. Steply receives only whether your subscription is active. It does not receive your payment details or your Google account.

Permissions

  • Activity recognition. To read the step sensor. Steply asks for this with an explanation before the system dialog.
  • Notifications (optional). To show the ongoing step count.
  • Foreground service (health). To keep counting while the app is in the background, and show the ongoing notification.
  • Internet and network state, advertising ID. For ads and the subscription described above.
  • Billing. For the optional subscription.

Steply does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Backups

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Steply, so your step data is not copied to Google Drive by Android backup. Steply's CSV export is the only way to take a copy of your data out of the app, and only when you choose to export.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data removes your step history, hourly data, counter readings and settings from the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes its private data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

Steply is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
