Qibla Compass Privacy Policy

App: Qibla Compass (qiblacompass) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 14 November 2026

Summary

Qibla Compass is an offline Qibla finder, prayer timetable and Hijri calendar. The bearing, prayer times and Hijri date are calculated on the phone from the city or coordinates you choose. It has no account, no developer server and no INTERNET permission. Location is manual; the app does not use GPS or request a location permission.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps only settings in private preferences on this phone: the chosen city or custom coordinates, label and IANA time zone; calculation method, Asr school and high-latitude rule; Hijri day adjustment; 12/24-hour clock; manual magnetic declination offset; enabled prayer reminders, lead minutes and theme. Prayer times, Qibla bearing and Hijri dates are calculated on demand and are not stored as a separate record.

What leaves the device

Nothing is uploaded. The app declares no INTERNET permission and contains no developer server, advertising, analytics or tracking code, so it cannot send your content to the developer or an ad network.

Qibla Compass has no share sheet, no export and no network feature. Your saved place, method and declination are not sent to the developer.

Permissions

The app declares POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+ runtime permission for the prayer reminders you enable) and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (normal permission used only to re-arm reminders after a reboot, clock/time-zone change or app update). It declares an optional compass hardware feature but no location permission, and it does not request storage, camera, microphone, contacts or network access.

Like any Android app built with AndroidX, the merged manifest also contains the library's signature-level DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is used internally for broadcast receivers, is granted only to this app's own signature, and gives no access to your data.

Android backup and device transfer

Android automatic backup is turned off for this app (the manifest sets allowBackup=false and fullBackupContent=false). Its data-extraction rules exclude file, database, sharedpref and external domains from both cloud backup and device transfer. The developer has no server copy to restore.

Manual location and sensors

The app does not use GPS, network location or a location permission. It uses the city or coordinates you choose and the time zone you select. While the Qibla screen is open it reads the rotation-vector sensor, or the accelerometer and magnetometer on devices without one, to show a magnetic heading. The sensor stops when the screen is backgrounded. Magnetic declination is manual; the default 0° means the heading is magnetic north and can differ from true north by many degrees. The compass is a guide, not a surveying instrument, and a phone magnetometer is affected by magnets, metal, speakers and car dashboards.

Prayer-time and Hijri limits

Prayer times are approximate solar calculations, not an official timetable. Methods differ by a few minutes, a winter high-latitude Asr can differ by about five minutes, and high-latitude rules differ by community. Polar times that cannot be calculated are left blank, not invented. The Hijri date is arithmetic (tabular), not based on local moon sighting, and can be one day out; the adjustment setting is a user correction, not authority. Reminders are local and inexact and only the next three days are armed. Confirm important times with your local mosque or timetable.

Ads, accounts and purchases

The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no advertising, analytics or billing SDK. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any data from the app.

Children

Qibla Compass is not directed at children under 13. It has no accounts and no way to send information to the developer, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Data deletion

There is no saved prayer-history file to delete. Reset settings or clear the app's storage from Android Settings > Apps > Qibla Compass > Storage. Uninstalling the app deletes its settings and scheduled reminder records. There is no developer server copy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. The app itself does not need to change for a policy update.
