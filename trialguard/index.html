TrialGuard Privacy Policy

App: TrialGuard: Trial Reminder (com.coderhub.trialguard) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 7 November 2026

Summary

TrialGuard keeps a list of your free trials and reminds you before they turn into paid subscriptions. Your trials, notes and settings are stored on your phone. The developer does not run a server for TrialGuard, does not have an account system, and does not receive your trial data. TrialGuard does not cancel anything for you.

Information stored on your device

  • Trials. Service name, price, currency, trial length, billing cycle, start and cancel-by dates, cancel link, notes and status (active, cancelled or kept).
  • Reminders. The times when reminders are due and whether they were shown.
  • Settings. Theme, reminder timing, whether reminders are on, consent choices and, if you bought it, the subscription state.

This data is kept in the app's private storage. It is not uploaded anywhere. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for TrialGuard, so the data is not copied to your Google account backup.

Reminders and notifications

TrialGuard asks for permission to show notifications (Android 13 and later). Reminders are scheduled with Android's alarm system and are re-scheduled after your phone restarts, after the app is updated and after the clock or time zone changes. This happens entirely on your phone. TrialGuard does not run in the background otherwise.

Text you share into TrialGuard

When you share text to TrialGuard from another app, the text is read on your phone to fill in the trial form: the name, price, trial length, billing cycle and any web link. The text is not sent anywhere and is not stored apart from the trial you save.

Ads

TrialGuard shows a banner ad on the home screen and an optional rewarded video that hides ads for 24 hours. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it, and it loads ads only when that consent allows it. You can change your choice with the Privacy options entry in Settings, when it is shown. AdMob may use an advertising ID and your IP address to show ads, under Google's privacy policy.

Subscription

An optional monthly subscription removes the home-screen banner. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing, under Google's terms. TrialGuard receives only whether the subscription is active.

Network use

The only network use is the ads and the subscription check described above. Every other feature works offline.

Permissions

  • Notifications. To show trial reminders.
  • Exact alarms. To send reminders at the time you chose. If it is turned off, reminders still arrive within a short window.
  • Start at boot. To re-schedule reminders after a restart.
  • Internet and network state, advertising ID. For the ads and the subscription check described above.

Data deletion

Settings > Delete all data removes all trials, reminders and settings from the phone. Uninstalling TrialGuard also removes its data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

TrialGuard is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes
