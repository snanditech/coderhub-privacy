DocShift Privacy Policy

App: DocShift (com.coderhub.docshift) · Developer: CoderHub26 · Effective: 23 October 2026

Summary

DocShift is a document scanner and PDF tool. It works on your device: your scans, PDFs and photos stay in the app's private storage on your phone. The developer does not run any server for DocShift, does not receive your documents, and does not sell or share your data. DocShift has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics SDK of its own.

Camera and photos

  • Camera. DocShift asks for the camera permission only to take pictures of documents. Pictures are processed on your device.
  • Photos and files. When you import a photo or a PDF, Android's system file picker gives DocShift access only to the file you choose. DocShift does not scan your whole gallery or storage.

Document scanning and text recognition (Google ML Kit)

DocShift uses Google ML Kit for two features:

  • Document scanner. Edge detection, crop and filters come from Google's ML Kit Document Scanner, which runs inside Google Play services on your phone.
  • Text recognition (OCR). Copying text from scans uses an on-device text recognition model bundled with the app.

Images are processed on your device. They are not sent to the developer or to Google by DocShift for these features. ML Kit may send limited diagnostic and usage information to Google, such as device information, app information and performance metrics, under Google's ML Kit terms. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, not by the developer.

Information stored on your device

DocShift keeps these in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your scans, page images, PDFs, signatures and exported files;
  • your folders, document names, search and sort choices, and the Trash (deleted documents are kept there for 30 days, then removed automatically);
  • your settings (theme, language, scan and compression defaults, and similar);
  • backup settings, if you use backup (see below).

You can delete single documents, empty the Trash, or use Settings > Delete all data to remove everything DocShift has stored. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your phone.

Backup to a folder you choose

DocShift can save a ZIP backup of your documents, or an automatic backup, to a folder you pick with Android's system file picker. That folder can be on your phone or in a cloud app such as Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.

  • DocShift writes the backup file to the folder you chose. The cloud app you picked (if any) does the upload, under its own terms. DocShift never receives your cloud account details or passwords.
  • You can protect a backup with a password. The backup is then encrypted with AES-256 on your phone before it is written.
  • Your backup password never leaves your phone. If you turn on automatic backups with a password, DocShift stores the password encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore, so the scheduled backup can run.
  • DocShift does not turn on Android's own cloud backup for your scans. Scans are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

Information that leaves your device

  • Only what you choose to share or back up. When you share a PDF, image or text, DocShift hands the file to the app you pick. When you save a backup, it goes to the folder you pick. What happens next is up to that app or service.
  • Feedback email. The Send feedback option opens your own email app with the support address filled in. The message is written by you and sent by you. It may include the app version, your Android version and your device model, shown in the draft so you can see them before you send. Nothing is sent unless you send the email.

DocShift does not send your documents, scans or usage data to the developer.

Permissions

  • Camera: taking pictures of documents.
  • Notifications: declared by Android's WorkManager library, which runs the scheduled backup and Trash clean-up in the background. DocShift does not send you notifications of its own.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): saving exported page images to your gallery. On Android 10 and newer, DocShift needs no storage permission.
  • Internet and network state: the Google ML Kit libraries declare these permissions in their own code. DocShift itself does not make network requests for your documents.

Data deletion

You can delete documents at any time in the app. Documents in the Trash are removed automatically after 30 days. Settings > Delete all data removes everything DocShift has stored on your phone. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Children

DocShift is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
